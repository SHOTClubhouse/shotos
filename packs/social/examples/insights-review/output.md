## What the data cannot show
- **"Community gets 8 times the reach" is not supported.** The community total is lifted by a single post. The median for community (265) is lower than education (300). I will not write the 8 times claim.
- **Why the post spiked.** Reach alone does not show a cause. A hashtag, a share by a local page or a boost are all possible. I have no data on which, so I will not say it was the hashtag. Check whether anyone shared it or it was boosted.
- **Facebook.** Missing.
- **Sample.** Ten posts, with one outlier. This is thin evidence for a large change.
