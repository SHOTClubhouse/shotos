# publish-with-manifest

**Publish only through a frozen, approved manifest, in a fresh step, and read every post back live.**

The manifest lists the exact posts, platforms, times, assets and checksums. A named person approves it. Execution then happens apart from the writing, for approved items only, and each live post is checked against what was approved. This is control 8, two-session execution, applied to social media. It serves rule 1, Stop at the boundaries, and rule 3, Read back the real result.

## The problem it solves

A café's weekly posts are drafted in a chat, tweaked three times and scheduled by whoever is free. On Friday a post goes out with an earlier draft of the caption, a promotion price that ended on Tuesday, and a photo that includes a child whose parents had not been asked. Nobody can say which version was approved or by whom.

## What the agent does differently

It freezes the text and the asset checksums before anyone approves, so what is approved is what goes out. Execution starts from the manifest alone. If a checksum or a character differs, it stops. After each post it opens the live result and records what matched.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it for any post that goes to an audience, and especially for scheduled batches, paid promotion or anything involving children.

Do not use it while captions are still being chosen, or when no one will approve.

## Who it is for

- **Small business owner:** proof of what went out and who said yes.
- **Club committee:** an approval trail that satisfies a safeguarding lead.
- **Marketing lead:** protection against the wrong draft going live.
- **Volunteer:** a checklist when there is no scheduling tool.

## How to use it

> Here are the four approved posts for this week. Build the manifest with checksums, show me exactly what will go out, and wait for my yes. Then publish from the manifest in a fresh session and read each one back.

## Needs

A named approver and the final files. A scheduling tool or connector is optional. Without one, a person posts from the manifest and does the read-back.

## Tips

- Keep the manifest with the asset files so the fresh step can verify them.
- If you edit one word, make a new manifest. It takes a minute.
- Approve per item. A blanket yes hides the one post you did not read.

## Limits

- Checksums prove the file did not change. They do not prove the content is right; that is the approver's job.
- A platform may recompress an image. The read-back compares visibly and notes what the platform changed.
- The agent can only publish through a tool you have connected and authorised.
