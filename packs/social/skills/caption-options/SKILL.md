# Write caption options with facts checked

Stage: Operate. Rules served: 4 Claim only what you can show, 1 Stop at the boundaries.

## When not to use this

- The task is publishing. Use `publish-with-manifest` after a caption is approved.
- The brief has no facts to build on and the person wants the agent to make some up. Ask for facts.
- The image shows or names a child and consent is not recorded. Stop and ask; do not draft a caption that identifies the child.

## Steps

1. **Collect the inputs.** The photo or brief, the account and platforms, the voice (brand guide or a sample of past posts), the goal (inform, thank, invite), and the call to action if any, with its link.
2. **List the facts.** Write the facts you may use, each tagged with its source: given by the owner, visible in the photo, or from a link the owner supplied. Mark anything you would like to say but cannot source as "need" and ask for it. Do not guess species, scores, prices, dates, names, places or numbers.
3. **Check people and consent.** If the image shows or names anyone, ask whether they agreed to appear. For a child, require recorded parental or guardian consent that covers this platform and use. If it is missing, offer captions that do not identify anyone and recommend a different image.
4. **Write five options, A to E.** Make them different in approach, not just wording:
   - A: plain and informative.
   - B: a short story or moment.
   - C: a question that invites replies.
   - D: very short and punchy.
   - E: a thank-you or community angle.
   Use the owner's voice, write sentences with verbs and keep claims small and checkable.
5. **Add platform variants.** For each platform the owner uses, give a length-appropriate version of the favourite option, a hashtag set (relevant, specific, few; check what the platform currently recommends) and alt text that describes what is visible in plain words, not the marketing message. Ask the owner to confirm current length limits if they matter.
6. **Run the claim check.** In a table, list every factual claim in the options with its source. Remove or soften: superlatives ("best"), guarantees, health or safety claims, results that cannot be shown, testimonials the owner does not hold, and urgency that is not true ("only two places left" without a number). Record what you changed and why.
7. **Return choices and questions.** Ask the owner to pick or edit. Do not mark a caption approved. Use [template.md](template.md).

## Output

A filled [template.md](template.md): the facts list, options A to E, platform variants, the claim check and open questions.

## Checks

- Every claim in every option appears in the facts list with a source.
- The five options differ in approach.
- Alt text describes the image.
- No child is named or identifiable without recorded consent.
- Nothing was posted and no caption is marked approved.
