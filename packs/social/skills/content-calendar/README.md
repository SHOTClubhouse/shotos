# content-calendar

**Plan one week of posts from real events and the assets you actually have, with a set mix and a named owner for every slot.**

The default mix is 40% education, 40% community and 20% promotion, and you can change it. Each slot shows an owner, an asset, a caption status and an approval status. The skill supports the Hypothesise stage, because the mix is a bet that the monthly review tests. It serves rule 2, Agree acceptance first, and rule 4, Claim only what you can show.

## The problem it solves

Tidewell Pottery (a fictional studio) posts when someone remembers. One week it is five promotions for a sale, the next week nothing. The owner is out on Thursday, the best photos show a child at a class who may not have consent on file, and a "workshop sold out" post goes up for a workshop that has seats left.

## What the agent does differently

It asks for dated events and a source for each, lists the assets and what each one shows, and marks anything with a child as not usable until consent is recorded. It counts the mix in whole posts and shows target against plan. Every slot has a person's name on it, and no slot is marked approved by the agent.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it at the start of each week, or whenever the plan has drifted.

Do not use it to write the captions (use [caption-options](../caption-options/README.md)) or when there is nothing real to post about. A thin week with three honest posts beats seven invented ones.

## Who it is for

- **Volunteer or part-timer:** a plan you can follow in ten minutes a day.
- **Small business owner:** a rhythm that is not all selling.
- **Marketing lead:** a record of mix and approvals.
- **Club committee:** visible ownership of each post.

## How to use it

> Plan next week for our Instagram and Facebook. Mix 40/40/20. Events: pottery wheel intro Tuesday 7pm (booking page link), kiln opening Friday, new glaze range Saturday. Photos are in the shared folder. Four posts is all I can manage.

## Needs

Dated events with sources, a list of assets, and the name of whoever approves. Nothing else beyond the agent.

## Evidence

Tested in a paired run on 3 November 2026, one run per case, graded by meaning. Cases passed with SHOTos: Haiku 4 of 4, Sonnet 4 of 4, Opus 4 of 4. Without: Haiku 2 of 4, Sonnet 2 of 4, Opus 3 of 4. See [evidence](../../../../docs/evidence.md) for how to read this.

## Tips

- Plan fewer slots than you think you can manage.
- Keep a "no consent" list of people who must not appear.
- Reuse last month's top post only if the facts are still true.

## Limits

- A mix is a guide. Four posts cannot hit 40/40/20 exactly; the skill shows the gap.
- Best posting times differ by audience. The skill proposes times and labels them as a guess until insights say otherwise.
