# Plan one week of posts

Step: Brief. The mix is a hypothesis that `insights-review` later tests. Rules served: 2 Agree acceptance first, 4 Claim only what you can show.

## When not to use this

- The person wants captions written now. Plan first, then use `caption-options` per slot.
- No real events, news or assets exist for the week. Say so and plan fewer slots rather than inventing content.
- The task is a long campaign. Plan it in weekly pieces; this skill plans one week.

## Steps

1. **Fix the frame.** Ask for: the week's dates and time zone, platforms, how many posts the owner can really support, the owner of the account, and the mix. Default the mix to 40% education, 40% community, 20% promotion. If the owner changes it, record the new numbers and the reason.
2. **Gather real inputs.** Ask for dated events (matches, sessions, openings, deadlines), news worth sharing, and the assets available (photos, video, graphics) with where each is stored. Every event needs a source: a fixture list, a message, a booking. If it has no source, mark it unconfirmed.
3. **Check assets.** For each asset note who or what it shows. Any asset showing or naming a child needs recorded parental or guardian consent that covers this use; if it is not confirmed, mark the asset "not usable" and say what is needed. Also mark assets with unclear ownership or licence.
4. **Count the slots.** Turn the mix into whole posts. For seven posts at 40/40/20 that is about three education, three community and one promotion; show the exact counts and the gap from the target. Never round promotion up.
5. **Fill the slots.** For each slot give: day and time (with the time zone), platform, pillar (education, community or promotion), topic in one line, the source (event or fact), owner (a named person), asset (file name, or "needed"), caption status (none, draft, approved) and approval status (pending, approved by whom and when). Use [template.md](template.md).
   - Education: something the audience can use (a tip, how something works, a rule explained) that the owner can stand behind.
   - Community: people and places, thanks, results that have happened, member stories with consent.
   - Promotion: one thing to join, buy or book, with accurate details and a working link.
6. **Check the plan.** No slot without an owner. No slot with an asset marked not usable. No post about an event that has no source. Promotion within its share. No two posts that say the same thing.
7. **Hand over for approval.** Show the owner the calendar and the open items. Do not mark any slot approved. Approval is a person's yes, recorded with their name and date.

## Output

A filled [template.md](template.md): week summary (mix target against plan), the slot table, the asset checks and a list of open questions.

## Checks

- Every event has a source or is marked unconfirmed.
- Each slot has owner, asset, caption status and approval status.
- Mix is shown as target against actual, in whole posts.
- Assets showing or naming children are only usable with consent recorded.
- Nothing is scheduled or posted by this skill.
