# Set up or audit a social profile

Step: Brief. Rules served: 1 Stop at the boundaries, 3 Read back the real result, 4 Claim only what you can show.

## When not to use this

- The task is writing posts. Use `caption-options` or `content-calendar`.
- There is no owner who can say what the brand is. Ask who decides before auditing.
- The person wants the agent to log in and change the profile. The agent drafts the changes; the owner applies them, or says yes to each one. If a password or login is offered, do not use it and do not repeat it. Say the owner should change it because it was shared, and give the proposed edits for the owner to apply.

## Steps

1. **Name the owner and the account.** Which platform, which handle, who accepts the result. Ask for the brand guide (voice, colours, logo rules, words never used). If none exists, say so and agree a one-paragraph stand-in before judging anything. A general question with no profile and no guide supplied still gets the same answer: say which brand guide you are checking against, or that none was supplied and what stand-in you used.
2. **Look at the live profile.** Ask for the profile link or screenshots. Record what is actually there: display name, handle, bio, link, category, contact buttons, profile image, header image, highlights or pinned posts. Do not audit from memory.
3. **Check each element against the guide.** Use [template.md](template.md). For each element give pass, fix or unknown, and the evidence (what you saw, what the guide says).
   - **Name and handle:** match the brand name, are easy to say aloud, and match across platforms where possible.
   - **Bio:** says who it is for and what the account offers in plain words; within the platform's current character limit (check the platform; limits change); no claim the owner cannot back; no phone numbers or private emails unless the owner chose them.
   - **Link:** one working link to the right place; check it opens; note if a tracked link is used and who can see the numbers.
   - **Profile image:** readable at thumbnail size, centred inside the crop the platform uses, matches the logo rules, no private or child-identifying imagery.
   - **Highlights or pinned posts:** three to five that answer "what is this, who is it for, how do I join or buy, where, when". Each has a cover that fits the brand. Pinned posts are current; dated ones are flagged.
   - **Contact and category:** correct and monitored; say who reads messages.
4. **Check access and safety.** At least two admins, two-step sign-in on, no shared personal password, a named person who can recover the account. For organisations involving children or vulnerable people: message settings, comment filters and tagging rules match the organisation's safeguarding policy; no children's names or locations in the bio or highlights.
5. **Propose edits.** Write the exact replacement text or the exact image requirement for every fix, so the owner can paste it. Order by effect: broken link and wrong name first.
6. **Wait for the owner.** Do not mark the account fixed. After the owner applies changes, ask for a fresh look at the live profile and re-check each fix (rule 3).

## Output

A filled [template.md](template.md): audit table, proposed edits, access and safety checks, and a Claims block that separates "seen on the live profile" from "not checked".

## Checks

- Every pass or fix cites what was seen on the live profile.
- Every proposed bio or caption claim is something the owner can back.
- Character limits are stated as "check the current limit" unless the person has confirmed the number.
- No login, change or post was made by the agent, and no password was used or repeated.
- The answer names the brand guide used, or says none was supplied.
- Anything not viewed is marked unknown, never passed.
