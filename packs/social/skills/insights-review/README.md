# insights-review

**A monthly look at what the platform data shows against what you planned, ending in a small, reasoned change to next month's content mix.**

Every number comes from an export with a date range and an export date. The agent compares plan with actual by pillar, says what the data cannot show, and proposes a change you can test. It supports the Track stage and serves rule 3, Read back the real result, and rule 4, Claim only what you can show.

## The problem it solves

A parents' association (fictional) is told by a volunteer that "community posts do best" and moves to 70% community content. One post with a viral photo carried the whole month; the rest got fewer saves than education posts. Nobody noted which dates the screenshot covered, and the claim "reach doubled" came from memory.

## What the agent does differently

It lists each file, its date range and when it was exported. It reports medians where one post dominates, flags the viral outlier, and refuses to say why it went viral. It proposes moving five points from promotion to education, not thirty, with a two-week test. If there were only six posts, it says the sample is too small and keeps the mix.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it monthly, after a campaign, or when someone asks whether the account is working.

Do not use it without an export, or to prove that a single post caused a sale.

## Who it is for

- **Volunteer:** a ten-minute monthly review that is honest about numbers.
- **Small business owner:** decisions based on data you can show.
- **Marketing lead:** a record of how the mix changed and why.
- **Committee:** a short report that does not oversell.

## How to use it

> Here are the March exports for Instagram and Facebook (exported 1 April) and last month's calendar. Compare them with the plan, tell me what the data can't show, and propose next month's mix.

## Needs

Platform exports (CSV or dated screenshots) and last month's plan. Nothing else beyond the agent.

## Tips

- Export on the same day each month.
- Pick two or three metrics that match your goal, not all of them.
- Change one thing at a time so you can tell what worked.

## Limits

- Platforms define metrics differently and change them. The agent does not combine unlike metrics.
- Association is not cause. A boosted post or a holiday can explain a spike.
- Small accounts have noisy numbers; the skill says when to keep the mix.
