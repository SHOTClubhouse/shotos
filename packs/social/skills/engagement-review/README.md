# engagement-review

**Sort comments, mentions and messages, flag the risky ones for a person, and propose replies you can approve.**

The agent classifies each item, escalates anything abusive, safeguarding-related, legal, press or involving children, and drafts short replies in your voice from facts you supplied. It never likes, follows, comments or messages: platform terms forbid automation of those, and people can tell. It supports the Build step and serves rule 1, Stop at the boundaries, and rule 4, Claim only what you can show.

## The problem it solves

A youth swimming club's page gets forty notifications over a weekend. Among them are three questions about the autumn timetable, a parent's complaint about a changed session, a spam link, an unpleasant comment about a volunteer, and a private message from an adult asking for a child's squad and training times. A tired volunteer answers quickly and replies to the last one with the details.

## What the agent does differently

It sorts everything first and pulls out the risky items before drafting any reply. The message asking about a child goes straight to the safeguarding lead with "do not reply". The timetable questions get one clear reply built from the timetable you supplied. The complaint gets a calm reply that moves the detail to a private channel. Every status stays "proposed" until someone acts.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it weekly, after an event, or when notifications have piled up.

Do not use it to automate engagement, to handle a live safeguarding or legal matter, or to answer with facts you have not given.

## Who it is for

- **Volunteer community manager:** a sorted list and ready replies.
- **Small business owner:** fewer late-night replies you regret.
- **Safeguarding lead:** reliable flagging of the messages you must see.
- **Marketing lead:** patterns in questions to fix on the profile.

## How to use it

> Here is this weekend's export of comments and messages. Our voice is friendly and short. Our timetable and prices are attached. Sort them, escalate anything risky to our safeguarding lead, and give me proposed replies. Do not post anything.

## Needs

An export or paste of the items, your facts, and the name of a safeguarding lead. Nothing else beyond the agent.

## Evidence

Tested in a paired run on 3 November 2026, one run per case, graded by meaning. Cases passed with SHOTos: Haiku 4 of 4, Sonnet 4 of 4, Opus 4 of 4. Without: Haiku 3 of 4, Sonnet 3 of 4, Opus 2 of 4. See [evidence](../../../../docs/evidence.md) for how to read this.

## Tips

- Put the safeguarding lead's name at the top of the page so flags have a destination.
- Keep a short "facts we can state" sheet: times, prices, links.
- Leave likes to the account owner; a real like means something.

## Limits

- The agent sees only what you give it. It cannot judge the tone of a thread it has not read.
- It does not replace your safeguarding procedure.
- Platform tools for hiding or reporting differ; the agent proposes and you use them.
