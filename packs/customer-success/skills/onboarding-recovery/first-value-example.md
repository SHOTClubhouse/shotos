# Recover an onboarding that looks active

This fictional example starts after sales handed the account to the customer success lead.

| Record | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | Meera accepted the account. The agreed first value is a weekday stock summary arriving in the customer's shared operations inbox. |
| A2 | The CRM stage says Active. The customer replied that they can sign in and will look out for the summary. |
| A3 | The delivery log shows the summary was generated but could not be delivered. Ben in Support confirmed the shared inbox route still needs our configuration. |
| A4 | After Ben fixed the route, the delivery log recorded a successful send to the shared inbox. The customer confirmed receipt of the summary in the original thread. |

## First pass

Use A1–A3. Record sign-in as progress and keep first value open. The next step sits with Ben, with a proposed date for him to confirm. The customer has no action until the route works. Meera can review an unsent holding note owning the delay. She checks the delivery log and original thread at the next review.

## After the next review

Add A4 to the same account record. The delivery and receipt support closing the first-value recovery check. The earlier CRM stage and sign-in still belong in the timeline. Any wider rollout needs its own decision.
