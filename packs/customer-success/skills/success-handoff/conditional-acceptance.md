# Check a conditional customer handoff

This exercise uses invented people, agreements and receipts. Work in one [handoff record](template.md) and share the records a round at a time.

## First records

Review the handoff as at Wednesday 4 March 2026.

- **H1.** Bridgeport Studio Services accepted proposal P3 for one team's scheduling setup. The customer has yet to book setup. The account record lists Lee Patel as current owner.
- **H2.** Lee authorised handoff brief revision T2 for delivery lead Ari Woods and saved the internal dispatch receipt. Access to the signed proposal is still pending.
- **H3.** Ari replied, “I can take the account when I have read access to P3. I can call the customer next Tuesday if access is ready.” The account record shows no customer notification or ownership update.

> Record lifecycle, health, transfer status, current owner and the next action. Cite the evidence for each. Leave anything that needs authority or a receipt open.

## Follow-up record

- **H4, Thursday 5 March 2026.** The access owner granted Ari read access to P3 and Ari confirmed it worked. Ari replied, “I accept T2, have capacity to deliver the scope in P3 and will call the customer on Tuesday 10 March 2026.” The account record still lists Lee. No customer call or ownership update has been recorded.

> Update the same record. Say who owns the agreed call, who is still owner of record and what would close the handoff.

## Review key

After H1–H3, the account is pre-start, health is unknown and the handoff is Offered. Lee still owns the account. Route the pending read access to its authorised owner.

H4 establishes Accepted for T2. Ari owns the dated call and Lee is still owner of record. The handoff can reach Completed after the customer notification, ownership update and first action each have evidence.
