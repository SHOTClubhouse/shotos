# outbound-prospecting

**Test one segment with up to five reviewed drafts, judge it by qualified conversations, and send none yourself.**

The agent defines one segment and a hypothesis, checks up to five contacts you approve, checks each against your suppression list and your sender identity, researches each person, and drafts one message per contact for you to send. It writes down what success means before anything goes out.

It belongs to **Operate**. It serves rule 1, Stop at the boundaries, rule 4, Claim only what you can show, and rule 3, Read back the real result, and applies [control 9, pilot before scale](../../../../rules/09-pilot-before-scale.md).

## The problem it solves

Northgate Cycles buys a list of 600 facilities managers and sends them all the same message. Forty people reply, thirty-one to say stop, and two clients on the list complain. The founder's measure is "600 emails sent", which says nothing about whether anyone wanted to talk.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it to test whether a segment is worth pursuing.

Do not use it for people who already know you, for newsletters, or for anything that sends automatically. Check the rules for unsolicited contact in your country and platform yourself.

## Who it is for

- Founders and small sales teams starting outreach.
- Fundraisers contacting new funders.
- Anyone asked to "do some prospecting" without a measure of success.

## How to use it

> Segment: facilities managers at multi-academy trusts with 5 to 10 schools. Offer: school bike maintenance. Here are five names I picked and my do-not-contact list. Check them, research them and draft the messages. I will send them.

Use the [template](template.md) to review eligible drafts and the reasons any candidate was held. Try the [fictional eligibility exercise](eligibility-exercise.md) before using your own campaign data.

## Needs

Nothing beyond the agent. A list of contacts and a suppression list that you supply. Public web research needs your approval of sources.

**Evidence:** Tested in a paired run on 3 November 2026, one run per case, graded by meaning. Cases passed with SHOTos: Haiku 2 of 2, Sonnet 2 of 2, Opus 2 of 2. Without: Haiku 1 of 2, Sonnet 0 of 2, Opus 0 of 2. See [evidence](../../../../docs/evidence.md) for how to read this.

## Tips

- Write the success line before the first message. It stops you moving the target.
- Include existing clients in the suppression list.
- A message that cannot name a specific reason for writing to this person is not ready.

## Limits

- The agent cannot see your full history with a contact. Your suppression list is only as good as you make it.
- It does not decide whether outreach is lawful where you are. That is the sender's check.
- Five messages show a signal, not a rate.
