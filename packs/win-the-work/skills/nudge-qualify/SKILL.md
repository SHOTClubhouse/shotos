# NUDGE qualify

Decide whether an opportunity deserves a proposal, more discovery or a polite exit, using five questions answered from evidence rather than hope.

Stage: Scope (and used again before committing to a proposal). Rules served: 4 (claim only what you can show) and 1 (stop at the boundaries).

## When not to use this

Skip it for opportunities that are already signed and in delivery, and for tiny requests that cost less to do than to score. Do not use it to rank people; it scores an opportunity.

## The five questions

| Letter | Ask | Evidence that counts |
|---|---|---|
| Numbers | What do we gain and what does it cost us (time, money, opportunity)? What does the buyer gain and spend? Is the benefit mutual? | A stated budget, a price, a cost they have quoted for the problem, our own cost estimate. |
| Understand | What is their challenge in their words? What does it cost them not to solve it? | Quotes or notes from a meeting, their documents, a stated deadline or penalty. |
| Definition | Is the work clearly defined? Can we deliver it in the time and budget? | A written scope, dates, our capacity check, dependencies named. |
| Gatekeepers | Who can say yes, who can say no, and what does each want personally? Positive and negative. | Names, roles, what each has said or done, any past decision they made. |
| Elevate | What sets us apart, and what value do we add above the competition (including doing nothing)? | A named alternative and a specific, checkable difference. |

## Steps

1. **Gather what exists.** Read the notes, emails and documents the person points to. Do not research outside them unless asked; say what you did not look at.
   Name the opportunity owner only when the supplied evidence identifies them. Otherwise write "owner not identified" in the scorecard; the SHOTos creator or signed-in account is not the deal owner.
2. **Score each question** 0 to 3: 0 unknown, 1 weak or hearsay, 2 reasonable with some evidence, 3 strong and confirmed. A question with no evidence scores 0 and is labelled **unknown**, never a guess. Write the evidence next to each score and say whether it is confirmed, reported, or your inference.
3. **Separate pursuit from a commitment.** A 0 or 1 on Numbers, Definition or Gatekeepers blocks a priced proposal or commitment until the gap is cleared. It does not automatically rule out the next discovery call. Name the missing fact and the person or event that could resolve it. A negative gatekeeper with no plan is a blocker.
4. **Recommend the next use of time.** Go means pursue the next bounded step: a credible buyer or route in, a real problem, a plausible funding route and a timely action are present. This can be a procurement check or a proposal, depending on what is known. Pause means there may be a fit but no useful near-term action until a named blocker or future event clears. Walk away means there is no credible mutual benefit or route to a viable deal, the work cannot be delivered, or the contact is only gathering competitive information. A total of 11 or more often supports go; under 6 after discovery often supports walk away. These are prompts to examine the evidence, not automatic verdicts. A low Elevate score, an unknown budget amount or an unconfirmed signer does not by itself downgrade an active opportunity. Explain any departure from the score guide and keep proposal and approval risks visible. The owner may set different lines.
5. **Name the next question.** Ask one named person about the uncertainty that most affects the decision or next step, even if another question has a lower score. Do not send it.
6. **State the cost of being wrong.** One line: what happens if the opportunity is pursued and the unknowns turn out badly.

## Output

Use [template.md](template.md): a scorecard with evidence and status per question, the recommendation, the blockers and the next question. Save with the deal, for example `.work/deals/<name>/nudge.md`.

## Checks

- Every score has evidence beside it, or the word "unknown" and a score of 0.
- Inferences are labelled as inferences.
- The recommendation follows from the evidence and the next step; any departure from the score guide is explained.
- Exactly one next question, addressed to a named person.
- Nothing was sent, and no figure was invented.
