# Buyer review pack

## Draft invitation

- Buying outcome and scope
- Deliverables and acceptance checks
- Budget authority, buyer owner and authorised issue channel
- Response deadline, timezone and clarification process
- Buyer dependencies, supplier obligations and exclusions
- Governing revision and amendment record

The scored weights total 100%. Record the published approach to missing evidence, rounding, ties and clarifications. Name the panel, conflict owner and award authority.

## Panel scorecard

- Supplier reference and response revision
- Reviewer and any conflict declaration
- Approved rubric revision
- Evidence access or clarification still open

Keep eligibility separate from weighted scoring. Record individual reviews and the panel's agreed rationale against the published rubric.
