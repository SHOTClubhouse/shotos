# Write a request for proposals

**Stage:** Hypothesise. **Rules:** 1 Stop at the boundaries, 2 Agree acceptance first, 4 Claim only what you can show.

## Context and handoff

Start from the buying need, budget authority, constraints, proposed procurement route and the organisation's review process. Use `capture-the-problem` if the buying outcome is unclear and `decision-brief` if the sponsor needs to choose a route. Use `bid-response` when responding as a supplier.

## When not to use this

Skip when replying to a tender, running an informal market enquiry or making the award.

## Steps

1. **Define the outcome.** State what must improve, what is in scope, dependencies, deliverables and how the buyer will accept them. Distinguish verified requirements from proposed requirements. Identify the authority to issue the request.
2. **Make responses comparable.** Specify the response format, assumptions, pricing breakdown, timelines, accessibility or support requirements when relevant, and the permitted clarification channel. Use one shared route for clarification and amendments. Flag any procurement or policy question for the buyer's qualified owner.
3. **Separate eligibility from scoring.** Identify mandatory pass/fail gates, then propose scored criteria with weights totalling 100% and what evidence earns a score. Give evaluators the same rubric, price assumptions and response evidence references. Before issue, specify how missing information, rounding and ties will be handled under the buyer's process. Ask the buyer to agree criteria, assign the evaluation panel and resolve conflicts of interest before issue. After issue, use the published criteria. Send proposed amendments or requests to cure a failed gate to the procurement owner for the published clarification process. Record their decision and use the same approved route for all affected suppliers.
4. **Check before issue.** Walk an eligible and an ineligible fictional response through the rubric. Use the [booking tool exercise](review-exercise.md) to check what the panel would do with a missing export. Check that mandatory gates cannot be masked by a high score, dates agree and questions elicit comparable evidence. Record the owner, exact draft revision, approval and issue as separate states.

## Output and checks

Use the [buyer review pack](template.md) to draft the RFP, supplier response schedule and scoring rubric. The panel can reuse the blank scorecard after issue with the approved rules and response evidence. Include unresolved authority, scope and timeline decisions. Do not contact suppliers, issue the request or choose a winner.
