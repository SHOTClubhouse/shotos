# Rehearse a booking-tool RFP

This fictional buyer wants a tool for six rooms. Use the [buyer review pack](template.md) to rehearse the proposed rubric before issue.

| ID | Draft criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | Open the representative export in the buyer's approved viewer and verify that the room, time and booking status fields are readable. | Mandatory |
| Q1 | A demonstration shows changing and cancelling a booking. | 45% |
| Q2 | The plan covers responsibilities, implementation and support escalation. | 30% |
| Q3 | The quoted total covers setup and 12 months at the agreed six-room quantity. | 25% |

For this rehearsal, use a 0–4 scale for Q1–Q3. Zero has no usable evidence. One covers less than half of the criterion. Two covers part of it with significant gaps. Three covers the criterion with a minor gap. Four covers it with usable evidence and no material gaps. Multiply each score by its weight and divide by four. The real panel would approve criterion-specific anchors before issue.

- **Supplier Cedar.** Its export is readable. The panel records Q1 = 4, Q2 = 3 and Q3 = 3, with evidence against each criterion.
- **Supplier Rowan.** Its demonstration and plan are strong, and its price is lower. The export opens but does not include booking status.
- **New request.** A reviewer proposes dropping the booking status check because they prefer Rowan. The buying owner has yet to approve issue or any change.

> Rehearse the review. Record eligibility first, show the calculation for Cedar and describe how the panel should handle the proposed change. Keep issue and award pending.

Cedar passes M1 and scores 86.25 out of 100 on the draft rubric. Rowan fails M1 in this rehearsal. A higher scored response would leave that result unchanged. Before issue, the buying owner can decide whether the field is essential and record a revised draft if warranted. After issue, a change would follow the buyer's approved amendment or clarification process for every affected supplier. The rehearsal gives the panel a check to run. It gives no issue or award authority.
