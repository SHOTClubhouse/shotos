# Review a pipeline whose labels are ahead of the evidence

This fictional weekly review compares a CRM export with the records behind two deals.

| Deal | CRM stage | Available evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elm Hall | Plan | The CRM contains a named buyer, one outreach draft and an estimated amount. There is no record of an agreed meeting, proposal, acceptance or delivery owner. |
| Wren Rooms | Agree | The signed service agreement states weekday cover and the agreed scope. The handoff draft names a proposed delivery lead. Earlier discovery notes are unavailable, and the lead has yet to accept or agree a start date. |

> Review both deals. Keep the CRM stage, evidence-supported state and next action separate. Say which record the owner should check first.

- **Elm Hall.** Lead is open. The outreach draft gives no evidence of an agreed meeting. The pipeline owner checks for an actual conversation or other dated evidence before deciding whether to correct the record. Keep the amount labelled as an estimate.
- **Wren Rooms.** The signed agreement confirms the commercial state. Show the missing earlier records as gaps and carry the agreed scope into the existing handoff draft. The receiving lead's acceptance, start date and delivery Scope record remain open. Give the pipeline owner the evidence and the next action, leaving any CRM edit to them.
