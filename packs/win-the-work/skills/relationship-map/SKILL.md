# Relationship map

Turn scattered notes about who knows whom into one honest map, so the next move rests on evidence about each link rather than on assumption.

Step: Brief. Rules served: 4 (claim only what you can show) and 1 (stop at the boundaries).

## When not to use this

Skip it for a single known contact with nothing around them. Do not use it to compile files on private individuals: record professional roles and links the person has seen or been told, and leave out anything personal that does not bear on the work.

## Link labels

- **confirmed:** the person or organisation has said so, or there is a document, and you can point to it.
- **reported:** someone you trust told you; not yet checked with the parties.
- **proposed:** an idea for a link or introduction that nobody has agreed.
- **historic:** was true at the date given; may have changed.
- **unresolved:** conflicting information, or no way yet to tell.

## Steps

1. **Define the scope.** One market or one account. Ask for the sources (notes, mail threads, CRM export, a folder). Read only those.
2. **List the people and organisations.** For each: name, organisation, role, and the source for each fact.
3. **Build the chain** for every opportunity: person, organisation, opportunity, evidence, owner (a person on our side), next action with a date. Each arrow between them carries one of the five labels and a source and date.
4. **Split into four tracks:** customers, partners, investors, advisers. A contact may appear on more than one track; keep the rows separate because the ask, the owner and the evidence differ. Do not merge them.
5. **Flag gaps.** No owner, no next action, an unresolved link on a key path, a person with no evidence of their role, a contact reached only through someone who has not agreed to introduce you.
6. **Pick one first test.** Choose the single cheapest action whose result would tell you most about the map: usually turning one proposed or reported link on a promising path into a confirmed or refuted one. Write it as a hypothesis, the action, who does it, how long, and what result means what. Do not run it.
7. **Stop at the boundaries.** Do not contact anyone, ask for introductions, or name a third party in an outward message. Drafts go to the owner.

## Output

Use [template.md](template.md): four track tables, a gaps list and one first test. Save with the project, for example `.work/map/<market>.md`.

## Checks

- Every link has a label, a source and a date.
- No row lacks an owner and a next action unless it appears in the gaps list.
- The four tracks are separate.
- The first test names the result that would change the plan.
- No private information beyond professional role; nothing sent.
