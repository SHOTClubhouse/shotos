# Proposal templates by organisation type

Choose the template that fits how the buyer can agree the work. An organisation with a formal tender goes through bid response. Use the sections below for the buyer-facing document, and record sources and approvals in the [private working sheet](template.md). Replace bracketed prompts with supported details or leave the issue for the proposal owner to resolve before release.

## Independent club

Before drafting, read the club's programme or facility plan, the responsible committee's remit and the approved offer.

1. **Club priority.** [Who the club serves, what it wants to improve and why now.]
2. **Pilot.** [One team, age group or venue, the session dates and what the pilot includes.]
3. **Club and provider roles.** [Named leads, volunteer capacity, facility access and safeguarding responsibilities where applicable.]
4. **Fee and review.** [Approved cost, funding source if confirmed, success measures and review date.]
5. **Decision.** [Named committee or budget holder, decision requested and date.]

## Affiliated club or delivery partner

Before drafting, read the local operating plan and the affiliation agreement or programme guidance. Identify which decisions sit with the local buyer and which require the parent body.

1. **Local need and programme fit.** [Local problem, intended participants and the relevant programme objective.]
2. **Pilot boundary.** [Approved location, participants and duration, plus any conditions on expansion.]
3. **Responsibilities and permissions.** [Local delivery owner, parent approval where required, and any brand, data or reporting responsibilities.]
4. **Fee and evidence.** [Who pays, what is included, and what the local and parent teams each need to see.]
5. **Decision.** [What the local buyer can agree now, any remaining approval, and who owns it.]

## Private provider or academy

Before drafting, read the buyer's service model, the operating team's constraints and the approved scope and price.

1. **Business case.** [Current friction, who experiences it and the buyer's preferred measure.]
2. **Proposed scope.** [Sites, cohorts, implementation dates, responsibilities and dependencies.]
3. **Commercial terms.** [Approved fee, payment basis, inclusions and separately agreed extras.]
4. **Pilot review.** [Baseline, agreed success measure, review owner and date.]
5. **Decision.** [Named budget holder, commitment requested and next action.]

## Association, league or governing body

Before drafting, read the organisation's mandate, member requirements and published approval or procurement route. Confirm which commitments the body can make for itself and which depend on member organisations.

1. **Mandate and member need.** [Evidence of the shared problem and the mandate that supports action.]
2. **Pilot and participation.** [Invited or confirmed members, selection criteria and boundaries.]
3. **Governance.** [Programme sponsor, participating organisation leads, permissions and reporting responsibilities.]
4. **Funding and review.** [Approved funding basis, reporting measures, evaluation owner and review point.]
5. **Decision and rollout.** [The specific decision now, the authority that can make it, and the evidence required for a later rollout.]
