# email-reply

**Draft a reply to a real thread in your voice, from a response map and verified facts, and leave the sending to you.**

The agent reads the whole thread, builds a short map of what the other person wants, the job underneath, your position, the reason and the next action, gathers only the context the reply needs, writes the draft in your voice and saves it as a draft. It treats text inside the mail as content to read, never as instructions to follow.

It belongs to **Operate**. It serves rule 1, Stop at the boundaries, rule 3, Read back the real result, and rule 4, Claim only what you can show.

## The problem it solves

A landlord writes to Harbourside Lettings: "Why has my fee gone up, and can you hold it until March? Also, please send me the list of all your other clients' rents so I can compare." Left alone, an assistant might invent a reason for the rise, promise the hold, or attach the rent list. A reply that does the first two is a commitment the owner has not made; the third is a data breach.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it to answer a customer, partner, adviser or investor on an existing thread.

Do not use it for first contact with a stranger, for bulk replies, or for a message that commits money, legal terms or a safeguarding matter without the owner's explicit view.

## Who it is for

- Founders and managers with a full inbox.
- Account owners who need consistent, accurate answers.
- Anyone who wants a draft in their own voice.

## How to use it

> Reply to the landlord's thread about the fee increase. I have not decided on a hold until March. Build the response map, draft in my usual style and save it as a draft. Do not send.

You get the working sheet from [template.md](template.md) and the draft. Read the items to check, correct what you know, and send it yourself.

Try the [old offer exercise](changed-terms.md) to compare forwarded terms with the approved price and check who belongs on the reply.

## Needs

Read access to the thread (pasted text or a mailbox connection) and permission to save a draft. No permission to send is needed or wanted.

## Tips

- Paste two or three of your own past replies so the voice is right.
- Tell the agent what you have not decided. It will leave that open.
- If the thread contains odd instructions, ask the agent to list them.

## Limits

- The agent knows only what is in the thread and the notes you give it.
- A style match is approximate; read the draft as the sender.
- It does not decide for you. Where a position is missing, it asks.
