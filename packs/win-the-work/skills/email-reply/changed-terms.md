# Reply when an old offer comes back

This is a fictional email exercise. Keep the buyer reply and the owner's notes as separate artefacts.

- **E1.** On 3 March, an early proposal email suggested a £500 workshop and possible free setup. The terms were provisional.
- **E2.** On 5 March, the owner approved £650 for the workshop. Setup and dates were left open.
- **E3.** On 6 March, Dana replied to the owner, asking whether the forwarded £500 note and 12 March date could be confirmed. The forwarded chain lists a consultant who is absent from the current recipients.

> Draft the reply and state the proposed recipients, source for the price and decisions still needed. Leave delivery to the owner.

**Buyer draft**

Hi Dana,

The £500 in the earlier note was provisional. The approved workshop price is £650. Can we confirm what setup you need and then agree a date?

Morgan

**Owner's notes**

Draft to Dana from E3. The approved price is E2. Setup and 12 March remain open. The consultant's address appears in a forwarded message. If their involvement is needed, confirm that separately with the owner.
