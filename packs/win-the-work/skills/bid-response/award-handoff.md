# From bid to delivery

This fictional training-room support tender shows how to use the same deal record through submission, award and handoff. Start with the [bid record](template.md).

| Record | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| B1 | Amendment A2 governs. M1 requires an insurance certificate valid at submission. Q2 scores the support plan with a 150-word limit. The team has a delivery reference, an expired certificate and an unapproved proposal for weekend cover. |
| B2 | The insurance owner supplied a certificate valid at submission. The pricing owner approved weekday-only support. The reviewer accepted revision R3 with the approved price, current certificate and a 142-word Q2 answer. |
| B3 | The named submission owner authorised R3. The portal receipt identifies R3 and records receipt before the deadline. |
| B4 | The buyer issued a preferred-supplier notice subject to contract. It requested weekend cover during mobilisation. |
| B5 | Both parties signed agreement C1. It includes weekday support across three rooms and a site access dependency before mobilisation. Weekend cover is excluded. |
| B6 | The current account owner authorised a handoff. The named receiving lead has the agreement and the first action to confirm site access, and has yet to accept the handoff. |

## How the record changes

1. With B1, mark **Hold** and route the insurance and pricing gaps to their owners. Draft Q2 from the verified reference.
2. With B2, record reviewer acceptance of R3. With B3, add submission authorisation and the separate receipt. Keep R3 attached to the deal.
3. With B4, record the conditional award. The weekend request needs a commercial decision, and delivery scope remains open.
4. With B5, link C1 as the controlling agreement. Record weekday support, weekend exclusion and who must confirm site access. Use those terms in Success Handoff.
5. With B6, record transfer as **Offered** if dispatch is evidenced. Otherwise it remains **Prepared**. The current owner retains responsibility until the named receiver accepts. Site access and mobilisation still need evidence.

The bid, contract and handoff share a record. Each has its own evidence and owner.
