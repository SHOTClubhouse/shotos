# Review a pilot claim before it goes public

A fictional service team is preparing an announcement. The reviewer is given the draft, the pilot report and the customer approval record.

| Record | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| P1 | The dated pilot report covers 20 support requests at one site. Median response time moved from 40 to 30 minutes across the two recorded windows. |
| P2 | The customer approved use of its name in a private sponsor briefing only. |
| P3 | Public draft R4 says “Response times are 25% faster across our network” and includes the customer's name in a caption. |

> Audit R4 for its intended public audience. Give the exact edit that would bring the metric within the evidence. Name any use permission still needed.

The report supports “Median response time fell from 40 to 30 minutes in the one-site pilot.” It gives no network-wide result. The customer name in the caption remains blocked for this audience until the owner has public-use permission. Recheck the revised wording and caption on the exact public draft.
