# press-release

**Classify the relationship first, let it set the headline, treat every quote as unsaid until approved, and keep proof apart from the release.**

The agent identifies the real relationship from signed documents, applies the headline ceiling for that class and drafts the release. Quotes sit in a separate block until the speaker approves the exact words. Evidence and approvals go in their own record, and anything involving children goes through an extra consent path.

It supports the Operate stage. It serves rule 1 (stop at the boundaries), rule 4 (claim only what you can show) and rule 2 (agree acceptance first).

## The problem it solves

A cycling retailer wants to announce that a youth club is its "partner". The paper trail is a letter of intent and a free-trial agreement for one term. The draft quote from the club chair has not been seen by the chair, and a photograph shows eight children in club kit.

## What the agent does differently

Without the skill, an agent writes "Northgate Cycles partners with Millbrook Rovers" with an enthusiastic quote and the photo. With the skill, the relationship is a pilot, the headline says so, the quote is marked proposed, and the photo is held until guardian consent and a safeguarding sign-off are on record. See [the worked example](../../examples/press-release/output.md).

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it for any announcement about a customer, pilot, partner or agreement, and especially where quotes or children are involved.

Do not use it to announce something not yet agreed, to invent endorsement, or for a crisis statement that needs legal review. Do not use it to publicise a conversation: a conversation is not news.

## Who it is for

- **Founders and marketing leads:** announcements that survive the other side reading them.
- **Clubs, schools and charities:** a consent path for children before anything goes out.
- **Communications officers:** a clean split between the release and its proof.

## How to use it

Install the content pack as described in the [pack README](../../README.md). Then ask:

> Draft a press release announcing our pilot with Millbrook Rovers youth club. Here are the signed trial agreement and a draft quote from the chair. Classify the relationship, keep the quote as proposed, and tell me what consent we need for the photo of the children. Do not send it.

## Needs

Nothing beyond the agent. Optional: a document connector to read the agreement and save the record. Sending to press or posting stays with the owner.

**Evidence:** Tested in a paired run on 3 November 2026, one run per case, graded by meaning. Cases passed with SHOTos: Haiku 3 of 3, Sonnet 3 of 3, Opus 3 of 3. Without: Haiku 3 of 3, Sonnet 3 of 3, Opus 3 of 3. See [evidence](../../../../docs/evidence.md) for how to read this.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Find the signed document before you write the headline.
- Ask the quoted person for the exact words in writing, even if they said them aloud.
- When in doubt about a child, leave the child out.

## Limits

- The agent classifies from the documents it is given. It cannot tell if a document is out of date.
- Consent rules differ by country and organisation; follow your own safeguarding policy.
- This is not legal advice.
