# Edit a creator's draft and keep their voice

Improve a creator's draft without replacing their voice, and hand it back with every change visible and the final decision theirs.

**Stage:** Operate. **Rules served:** rule 1 (stop at the boundaries), rule 3 (read back the real result) and rule 4 (claim only what you can show).

## When not to use this

Do not use it to ghost-write from nothing (use written-content), to rewrite in a different voice, or when the creator wants a full rewrite. Do not use it to post on the creator's behalf.

## Steps

1. **Agree the brief.** Name the creator, the channels, the audience, what they want help with (clarity, length, structure, mistakes) and what must not change. The creator is the one who accepts the work.
2. **Read the draft for voice.** Note the creator's habits: sentence length, vocabulary, humour, spelling, punctuation. Write three to five of these down as the voice notes and follow them.
3. **Set the keep target.** Aim to keep 85 to 95 per cent of the original wording. Count by words kept unchanged. If a fix needs more, explain why and ask before going past it.
4. **Edit lightly.** Fix errors, tighten loose sentences, move a buried point forward, cut repetition. Prefer deleting to rewriting, and prefer the creator's own word to a smarter one. Do not add claims, jokes or opinions the creator did not write.
5. **Keep a change log.** Show the original and the edit side by side for every change that is more than a typo, with a one-line reason. Provide a tracked-changes or comparison version.
6. **Run the fact check.** List every factual claim, number, name, date, quote and link. Check each against a source or mark it "creator to confirm". Never fix a fact silently; flag it.
7. **Run the consistency check.** Check names, spellings, tense, tone, the creator's earlier positions as supplied, and the brief. Flag a contradiction rather than choosing a side.
8. **Run the risk check.** Flag anything that could cause harm: defamation of a real person, unsupported health or money claims, undisclosed sponsorship or affiliate links, third-party content used without rights, personal data, children, and anything that misstates what a company or person said. Recommend a fix or removal and let the creator decide.
9. **Package per channel.** Prepare each channel's version (for example newsletter, short post, caption, video description) to its length and format limits, using the same edited text as far as possible. Do not add hashtags or links the creator did not choose.
10. **Hand back for approval.** Give the creator the edited draft, the change log, the keep rate, the check results and the channel versions. The creator approves the final. Scheduling or posting happens only on their yes.

## Output

The edited draft, a side-by-side change log, the keep rate with how it was counted, the fact, consistency and risk results, and one version per channel. Marked "waiting for the creator's approval".

## Checks

- The keep rate is stated and within 85 to 95 per cent, or the reason it is not is given.
- Every change beyond a typo is logged with a reason.
- No fact was changed without being flagged to the creator.
- Risks are listed with a recommendation, and the creator decides.
- Each channel version fits its limits.
- Nothing was posted, scheduled or sent.
