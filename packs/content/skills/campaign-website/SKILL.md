# Build a campaign website

**Stage:** Operate. **Rules:** 1 Stop at the boundaries, 2 Agree acceptance first, 3 Read back the real result, 4 Claim only what you can show.

## Context and handoff

Read the campaign hypothesis, audience, approved offer and proof, primary action, current site or builder, approved destination and asset rights. Use `research-and-assemble` to support claims, `image-create` for a graphic and `claims-audit` before external review.

Name the approved source brief (file, revision and owner) in the first line of the output and cite it for every claim. Anything not in the brief is a question for the owner. If `.work/capabilities.md` exists, read the section for this runtime. If a browser tool is missing, say so first, build the static preview and write the phone, desktop and keyboard checks as a manual script and mark the check pending.

## When not to use this

Skip for redesigning an entire product, configuring downstream lead automation, or deploying an already approved site.

## Steps

1. **Set one action and its success measure.** Agree who the page is for, what promise is approved and what a useful visit does. An unfinished form or placeholder destination needs a preview label.
2. **Choose the smallest page that answers the buyer's questions.** Lead with audience and offer, show proof and answer the key objection. Use approved graphics and retain asset rights and alt text. Keep copy editable and meaningful at narrow widths.
3. **Build a local or approved preview.** Use the owning site's stack or builder. Keep fake data marked as fictional. Until the page owner and the relevant data owner confirm the destination, permitted fields and handling in an approved record, use a local demonstration that makes no external writes. Do not add tracking by default.
4. **Verify what a visitor can do.** Open the preview at phone and desktop widths. Check keyboard order and focus, contrast, headings, image alternatives, broken links and CTA text. If a form is authorised, test validation and actual delivery on approved test data. If no delivery path was tested, report it unverified.
5. **Hand over for review.** Link the exact revision and preview, proof of checks, any unresolved claims or destinations, and the next owner. Record launch approval, publishing and live verification as separate states.

## Output and checks

Deliver a preview, an asset and claim record, and an observed test result. A generated page is only a draft until the owner approves the release and the primary action is verified in the intended environment.

**Reviewer check:** open the preview at phone width, press the primary action and confirm it does what the handover says. Pick one claim and find it in the cited brief.
