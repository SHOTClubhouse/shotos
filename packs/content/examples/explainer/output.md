# Explainer: where does my deposit go?

**Class:** external (the reader is a tenant outside the organisation). **Reader:** a new tenant. **After reading, they can:** say where the deposit is held, when it comes back and how a deduction is worked out.

## Draft

Your deposit is not Harbourside's money. When you pay it, we put it in a government-approved deposit protection scheme within 30 days. The scheme holds it until your tenancy ends.

**What happens at the end.** You give your keys back. We compare the property with the check-in report you signed. If nothing is damaged beyond normal wear, we ask the scheme to return the whole deposit to you.

**Worked example (invented figures).** Deposit: 900 pounds. At check-out, the oven needs a professional clean that costs 80 pounds. The check-in report records the oven as clean. We propose a deduction of 80 pounds and send you the quote. If you agree, you receive 820 pounds. If you disagree, the scheme's free dispute service looks at both sides and decides.

**Where to check.** The scheme sends you a certificate. It shows the scheme's name and the amount held.
