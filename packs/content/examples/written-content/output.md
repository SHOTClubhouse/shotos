# One-pager: draft for review

**Owner who accepts it:** director. **Type:** one-pager. **Reader:** small landlords (1 to 5 properties); after reading, they should list a property before the current tenant leaves.

## Draft

**Empty weeks cost more than the rent you lose**

Situation: Most small landlords find a new tenant after the old one has left. Across the 120 properties we managed last year, the average empty period was 19 days (our own records; illustrative figure for this example).

Problem: Each empty day is a day of lost rent while the mortgage, insurance and bills carry on.

Evidence: In our records, properties first advertised after the tenant left stayed empty longer than those advertised before. (Owner to confirm the comparison from the sheet and give the numbers.)

Idea: Advertise while the current tenant is still in the property.

How it works: Give notice. Book photographs for the week before the tenant leaves. List the property with the move-in date. Arrange viewings with the current tenant's agreement.

What to do next: Check the notice date on your next tenancy and book photographs for the week before it ends.
