## Source letter

Dear parents,

Millbrook Rovers summer camp runs from Monday 28 July to Friday 1 August at Mill Lane playing fields. Drop-off is 8.45am and pick-up is 3.30pm. The cost is £65 per child for the week, or £15 per day.

Please book by 1 June. No refunds after 1 June unless the camp is cancelled.

Children need a packed lunch, a water bottle and shin pads. If your child has a medical condition, tell us on the booking form.

Thank you,
Carol, Club Secretary
