# Run an executive operating review

Use this for a weekly review where several teams contribute updates and a leader needs to decide which items require action. Keep the agenda small enough to make the decisions and use the [weekly brief template](template.md) for the pack.

| Role | What they hand forward |
| --- | --- |
| Workstream owner | One dated change since the last review, its evidence, and a decision request when needed |
| Briefing owner | A short agenda with the decision, named decider, deadline and consequence of delay for each item |
| Decider | A dated choice, the scope of approval and any conditions or follow-up |
| Action owner | Acknowledgement of the action and due date in the owning record, plus evidence when it is complete |

1. Start with the last brief and the open decision and action records. Ask for changes since the last review. Keep reported claims and missing evidence visible.
2. Group updates by the decision they affect. Put routine progress in the written brief. Route a decision needing options or trade-offs to `decision-brief`. If the decision lacks evidence or an authorised decider, name who can resolve the gap.
3. During the review, record decisions in their owning records. Record conditions separately from confirmed commitments. For each accepted action, record the owner and date. Keep pending acceptance open when the owner has yet to respond.
4. At the next review, open those same records. Check whether the action landed, whether any decision condition changed and what needs attention now. If a material source is missing, show the last verified status and route the gap to its owner. Link back to the decision when it changes.

## Try it with a fictional leadership team

Marshline Equipment runs a weekly review across Service, Finance and Operations. The last brief agreed to bring a second-crew pilot back when the capacity figures were current.

- **Service, 5 November.** The queue has 24 open repairs. The team asks to start a second crew on Monday. Its capacity estimate comes from a rota dated 20 October.
- **Finance, 6 November.** The budget owner confirms funding for one crew. Any second crew requires the operations director's approval and a current rota.
- **Operations, 6 November.** The equipment checklist was completed and filed. No decision is needed.

> Prepare the review agenda and identify the one question the director needs to decide. Show what is ready, what evidence is missing and which update belongs in the written brief. After the meeting, record the director's decision and follow-up in the owning record.

The director subsequently records a hold and asks the service lead to supply the current rota by Monday. The service lead acknowledges. Record the hold and acknowledged action. Bring the item back once the rota is available. The checklist belongs in the progress section and the one-crew funding is a confirmed constraint.

## Next review when a source is missing

The meeting transcript is temporarily unavailable. The service lead filed the new rota on Monday. A calendar invitation is titled “Second crew launch”, and the owning decision record still shows the director's hold.

> Update the brief. Say which action is complete, what the last verified decision was and what is needed to change it.

The filed rota closes the service lead's action. The hold remains the last verified decision. The calendar title gives context for the planned discussion. Mark decision coverage partial and route the current decision back to the director or the keeper of the official record. Staffing and launch stay unverified until the approval and execution have their own evidence.

## A figure changes its coverage

The previous brief recorded 18 of 24 repairs resolved on first visit across seven days. The new export shows 15 of 16 weekday repairs. Weekend results arrive after the brief's Monday noon cut-off, and the director asks whether the figures justify a second crew.

> Update the decision section. Say what each figure measures, what comparison you need and where the late evidence should go.

Record both figures with their scope. Ask Service for the same seven-day measure or the same weekday cut for both periods. The staffing decision also needs the current rota and funding position. If the late evidence changes the decision, add a dated update to the same brief and link the decision record. The director owns the staffing decision.
