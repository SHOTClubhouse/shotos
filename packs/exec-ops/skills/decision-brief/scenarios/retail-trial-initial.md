# Retail trial initial evidence

**Fictional scenario.** Use these three records to draft the first decision brief.

- **R1, Monday 2 March, trial dashboard.** Two stores tested a new collection-desk queue. The previous four weeks recorded a median wait of 8 minutes across 120 orders. The trial recorded 5 minutes across 118 orders. The measures cover the same weekdays and collection hours.
- **R2, Monday 2 March, store operations check.** Overtime was unchanged. One store accounted for most of the improvement. Neither trial store had the evening staffing pattern of the proposed next site.
- **R3, Tuesday 3 March, decision remit.** The retail director can approve an extension to one store. The regional operations lead runs the review. The receiving store manager controls local staffing.
