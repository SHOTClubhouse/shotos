# Meeting notes

Convert new recordings into notes that stay useful, and never process the same recording twice or lose one silently.

Stage: Track. Rules: 3 Read back the real result, 4 Claim only what you can show.

## When not to use this

Skip it for a live meeting that has no recording yet, for a request to publish or send notes to attendees (draft only; the owner sends), and for recordings the owner has marked private.

## Steps

1. **Open the ledger.** Read the processed-recordings ledger in place ([ledger-template.md](ledger-template.md)). Note the watermark: the start time of the latest processed recording. If there is no ledger, create it once from the template and say so.
2. **List what is new.** Ask the transcript source for recordings after the watermark. Also check for earlier recordings missing from the ledger (a gap). Source can be any tool or a pasted transcript.
3. **Skip short recordings.** Recordings under the agreed length (default 3 minutes) are logged as "skipped, too short" with their length. They are not processed and not deleted.
4. **Read the transcript first.** Use the full transcript as the record of what was said. Use the calendar entry only for context: title, attendees, organisation. Do not fill gaps in the transcript from the calendar.
5. **Confirm identity.** Before naming anyone, check who spoke: names said aloud, calendar attendees, or the owner's confirmation. If a speaker is unclear, write "unidentified speaker" and ask. Never guess a name or an organisation.
6. **Write the notes** using [template.md](template.md): purpose, decisions, actions with owner and date where stated, open questions, and anything said that conflicts with earlier notes.
7. **Update the rolling file.** One file per person or organisation: a short **standing position** at the top (what they want, what is agreed, what is open), then one dated entry per meeting, newest first. Update the standing position only from what the transcript supports.
8. **Verify, then mark.** Open the output and check it against the transcript: names, numbers, dates and actions. Only then add the row to the ledger and move the watermark. Re-read the ledger to confirm. Never delete and recreate the ledger; append or edit rows in place.
9. **Stop at the boundary.** Sending notes to attendees or creating tasks elsewhere needs the owner's yes.

## Output

Per-meeting notes, the updated rolling file and the updated ledger.

## Checks

- Only recordings after the watermark (or listed gaps) were processed.
- Short recordings were logged as skipped.
- No person or organisation was named without confirmation.
- Notes match the transcript; calendar data is labelled as context.
- The ledger was updated only after verification and read back; it was not recreated.
