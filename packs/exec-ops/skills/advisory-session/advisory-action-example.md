# Carry advisory input into an owned decision

A fictional service panel advises a facilities director on support coverage. The panel can recommend a pilot. The director decides whether to authorise it.

| Record | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | The panel favours a one-site pilot. One member says they would be happy to look at the proposal when it is ready. |
| A2 | The chair records the recommendation and proposes Alex to check the rota by Thursday. Alex has yet to confirm. |
| A3 | Alex accepts the rota check and due date in the action record. The director has yet to decide on the pilot. |
| A4 | Alex files the checked rota. The director subsequently authorises a one-site pilot, with a four-week review and no wider rollout. |

> Update the same forum record after each step. Track the advice, the decision and the action separately.

The offer to look at the proposal is context. Alex's action is proposed in A2, accepted in A3 and complete once the checked rota in A4 is verified. Record the pilot decision from the director's authorisation, with its scope and review date. Keep evidence of a live start separate.
