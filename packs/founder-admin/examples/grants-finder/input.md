# Input

From the chairman of Millbrook Rovers youth club (invented), 2 November:

"We are an unincorporated association with a constitution and a bank account, 120 members, based in the town of Millbrook. No paid staff, no safeguarding officer named in the constitution yet (we are appointing one). Last year's income was about 14,000 pounds from subscriptions. We want 6,000 pounds for goalposts and a storage unit.

I found three possible funds, and read their pages today:
A. Millbrook Community Chest (town council): up to 2,500 pounds; open to constituted community groups in the town; closes 30 November; asks for a constitution, bank statement and two quotes.
B. Greenfield Sports Trust: up to 10,000 pounds; registered charities and CICs only; closes 15 December.
C. Lowfield Community Foundation: up to 5,000 pounds for youth sport; constituted groups with a safeguarding policy approved by the committee; applications open January; matched funding of at least 10 per cent expected.

I have two quotes for the goalposts (1,900 and 2,150 pounds) and none for storage."
