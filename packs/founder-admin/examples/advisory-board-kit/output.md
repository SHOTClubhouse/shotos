# Advisory board kit

**Company:** Northgate Cycles
**Owner:** the founder
**Purpose:** advice on winning school and council contracts, pricing a fixed-fee service and safeguarding for work in schools
**Size and term:** three advisers, 12 months, renewable by agreement
**Status:** Draft. Nothing sent or signed.

Two requests are not in the kit: equity of 0.5 per cent each, and naming the advisers on the website next week. Equity needs legal and tax advice and a decision by the company; the kit leaves the payment line "to be decided with legal advice". Names and photos go on the website only after each adviser has agreed in writing, and after the rules below are agreed with them.

## Roles
| Role | Gap | What they will do | What they will not do | Time | Reports to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chair (company) | Runs the board | Sets agenda, circulates papers, keeps action log | Not an adviser | 2 hours a quarter plus prep | Founder |
| Adviser: contracts | School and council contracts | Review bids, advise on buyer routes | Bind the company; speak to buyers on its behalf | 2 hours a month | Chair |
| Adviser: pricing | Fixed-fee pricing | Challenge the pricing model | Set prices | 2 hours a month | Chair |
| Adviser: safeguarding | Safeguarding in schools | Review policies and procedures | Sign off policies as the company's responsibility | 2 hours a month | Chair |

## Expectations
**Advisers:** attend quarterly meetings; reply to papers within 7 days; give honest views; keep company information confidential; declare conflicts (for example work for a competitor or a buyer).
**Company:** circulate papers 5 days before; share information needed to advise; report back on what was done with advice; not use an adviser's name without written agreement.

**Questions for the lawyer:** (1) Should advisers sign a short agreement, and what should it say about confidentiality and IP? (2) If the company later wants to give equity, what are the tax and legal steps? (3) What must be said to advisers who work for a buyer or a competitor?

## Meeting rhythm
Quarterly meeting of 90 minutes with a 20-minute monthly check-in. Agenda: decisions needed, progress since last time, one topic, actions. Papers circulated 5 days before; notes issued within 3 days; the chair keeps the action log.
