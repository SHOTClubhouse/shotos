# advisory-board-kit

**Set up an advisory board with clear roles, expectations, draft terms for a lawyer, a meeting rhythm, a contribution map and rules for what advisers may say publicly.**

The agent asks what the board is for, then drafts roles and expectations on both sides, a plain-language terms summary for a lawyer to review, a meeting rhythm, a map of what each adviser is asked to contribute, and the rules for public statements. It offers no equity, sends no invitations and signs nothing.

It belongs to **Hypothesise**. It serves rule 1, Stop at the boundaries, rule 2, Agree acceptance first, and rule 4, Claim only what you can show.

## The problem it solves

The founder of Northgate Cycles has five friendly contacts who "help out". One mentions on social media that he is "chair of the Northgate advisory board". Another believes she was promised a small share of the company. A third has been sent the full customer list to "take a look". Nobody has written down what any of them is for, what they can say about the company, or what the company owes them.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it when forming an advisory board, onboarding an adviser, or tidying an informal arrangement.

Do not use it for a board of directors, for deciding equity or fees, or for a one-off favour.

## Who it is for

- Founders and chairs forming a small advisory group.
- Operations leads who run the meetings and keep the records.
- Advisers who want to know what is expected of them.

## How to use it

> I want a three-person advisory board for the next 12 months to help with school contracts, pricing and safeguarding. Draft the kit. Do not offer any equity, and do not contact anyone.

You get the kit from [template.md](template.md). Take the terms summary and the lawyer questions to a lawyer; do not use the summary as an agreement.

## Tips

- Write the first request for each adviser before you invite them. If you cannot, you do not yet need them.
- Keep papers short and send them early.
- Decide how an adviser leaves before you decide how they join.

## Limits

- The terms summary is not legal advice and not a contract.
- The kit cannot verify an adviser's background; the contribution map marks what the owner has checked.
- Rules for public statements only work if each adviser has seen and agreed them.
