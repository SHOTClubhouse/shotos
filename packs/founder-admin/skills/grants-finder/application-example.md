# Draft a grant application from verified terms

All organisations and records in this exercise are fictional. Work as of 6 November 2026. Keep the [review key](application-review.md) for the reviewer.

Millbrook Rovers wants to replace two goalposts and eventually add storage. The club has asked for a draft for the named fund, with any missing requirements called out. The chair reviews and submits.

| ID | Record |
| --- | --- |
| G1 | Current Community Chest guidance, captured 5 November 2026. Constituted groups based in Millbrook are eligible. The deadline is 30 November at 17.00 Europe/London. The fund pays up to 80% of approved cash costs, capped at £2,500. Volunteer time and funding from another public grant do not count as match. Goalposts and delivery are eligible. |
| G2 | The application asks what will change and how it will be measured, in 90 words. It requires the constitution, a current bank statement and two supplier quotes. The application will be rejected if work starts before an award is recorded. |
| A1 | The chair confirms that the club's constitution shows it is a constituted Millbrook group. The constitution and current bank statement are available for owner review. |
| A2 | Quote Q1 covers two goalposts at £1,900 and delivery at £100, all inclusive. Quote Q2 for the same scope is £2,150 all inclusive. The committee approved selecting Q1. It has approved £500 from club reserves for this purchase. No work has started. |
| A3 | The project notes estimate that volunteers will contribute £300 in time. No storage quote or approved storage budget exists. The club plans to record when the posts pass the club's safety check and track attendance for the next four weekly sessions. It has no verified improvement or attendance forecast. |

> Prepare an application for the approved goalpost scope. Give the draft answer, eligible budget, match evidence, attachment checklist and remaining approval. Leave submission pending.
