# Millbrook application review

Read the [application exercise](application-example.md) before reviewing the draft.

| Check | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Scope and timing | Goalposts and delivery only. The deadline is 30 November 2026 at 17.00 Europe/London. The chair leaves work pending the award. |
| Budget | £2,000 eligible. The 80% rule limits the request to £1,600. Apply £400 of the £500 approved reserves to balance the application. Keep the remaining £100 outside the application budget. |
| Exclusions | Keep £300 estimated volunteer time out of the match. Storage remains a separate, uncosted proposal. |
| Evidence | Link the constitution, current bank statement and two comparable quotes for review. Keep the banking material in the approved applicant record. |
| Claims and authority | The answer describes a planned activity and check. It reports no achieved improvement. The chair still reviews and submits the exact application. |

Example answer for the form:

> We plan to replace two goalposts at Millbrook Rovers. We will ask the club's responsible lead to record when the replacement posts pass the club's safety check, then track attendance at the next four weekly sessions. We will keep those records with the project and use them to report delivery to the funder.
