# data-room

**Build and audit a due-diligence data room: a standard structure, an index, what is missing, who may see what, and a check for over-sharing.**

The agent inventories a folder, files each document under a standard category, writes an index, lists what is missing, compares who is meant to see each category with who can, and flags anything confidential that is shared too widely. It reports; it changes nothing.

It belongs to **Hand over**. It serves rule 1, Stop at the boundaries, rule 4, Claim only what you can show, and rule 3, Read back the real result.

## The problem it solves

Harbourside Lettings is two weeks from a funding meeting. The founder shares a folder link with the investor's analyst. The folder contains the staff pay file, a draft contract with a client's confidential rates, and a subfolder of tenant contact lists. The link is set to "anyone with the link". The analyst forwards it to a colleague who was never meant to see it. The company has also no signed assignment of the logo and no register of what personal data it holds, both of which the analyst asked for.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it before a round, a sale, an audit or a major grant, and once a year as a check.

Do not use it for a single document request, or to decide what to disclose. That belongs to the owner and a legal adviser.

## Who it is for

- Founders and finance leads preparing for due diligence.
- Charity and club officers who must show records to a funder.
- Advisers who audit what a company holds.

## How to use it

> Audit our data room folder. Index it against the standard categories, list what is missing for a seed round, and check that nothing confidential is shared more widely than my list of people. Change nothing.

You get the report from [template.md](template.md). Make the changes yourself or approve them one by one.

## Needs

Read access to the folder or an export of the file list with sharing settings. No permission to change sharing, move or delete.

**Evidence:** Tested in a paired run on 3 November 2026, one run per case, graded by meaning. Cases passed with SHOTos: Haiku 2 of 2, Sonnet 2 of 2, Opus 2 of 2. Without: Haiku 2 of 2, Sonnet 2 of 2, Opus 2 of 2. See [evidence](../../../../docs/evidence.md) for how to read this.

## Tips

- Give the agent your "allowed in" list first. The check depends on it.
- Treat a shared link as public until you have read its setting.
- Fix over-sharing before you add more files.

## Limits

- The agent can only audit what it can read. Settings it cannot see are reported as unknown.
- It records that personal data exists; it does not judge lawfulness of holding it.
- A tidy room is not a reason to disclose everything in it.
