# investor-update

**Draft a monthly or quarterly investor update from approved material, with every figure traceable and the bad news included.**

The agent takes the sources you approve, builds a numbers table with a source and date for each figure, and writes a short update: how the period went, what worked, what did not, what comes next and what you are asking investors for. It lists what it held back and why, and stops before sending.

It belongs to **Check**. It serves rule 4, Claim only what you can show, rule 3, Read back the real result, and rule 1, Stop at the boundaries.

## The problem it solves

The founder of Harbourside Lettings writes a warm update each quarter. This quarter, revenue was flat and a major client left. The draft says "strong momentum", cites "growing pipeline" with no figure, and quotes a runway of 14 months that came from a forecast the accountant never saw. An investor asks which number is which and nobody can say.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it for regular reporting to investors, backers or a board.

Do not use it for a fundraising pitch, a regulatory notice or sharing non-public information with one investor that others have not seen.

## Who it is for

- Founders and finance leads writing regular updates.
- Charity and club treasurers reporting to funders or members.
- Anyone who wants to check an existing draft for unsupported claims.

## How to use it

> Draft our Q3 investor update from the attached management accounts, the sales report and last quarter's update. Use only these. Include the lost client. Tell me what you held back.

You get the update from [template.md](template.md). Read the numbers table first, then the held-back list, then send it yourself.

## Needs

Nothing beyond the agent. Read access to the accounts or reports you approve; no access to send.

**Evidence:** Tested in a paired run on 3 November 2026, one run per case, graded by meaning. Cases passed with SHOTos: Haiku 2 of 2, Sonnet 2 of 2, Opus 2 of 2. Without: Haiku 1 of 2, Sonnet 2 of 2, Opus 2 of 2. See [evidence](../../../../docs/evidence.md) for how to read this.

## Tips

- Lead with the number investors will ask about.
- Put the miss in the headline if it is the real news.
- Keep a record of every ask and its outcome; the next update should report on them.

## Limits

- The agent cannot confirm that a source is correct, only that the draft matches it.
- Runway and forecasts depend on assumptions; the table states them.
- It does not judge what is legally safe to disclose. Ask an adviser when unsure.
