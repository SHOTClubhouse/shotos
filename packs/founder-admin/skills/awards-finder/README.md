# awards-finder

**Find free-to-enter awards, screen eligibility and terms, and prepare evidence-backed entries without paying or submitting.**

The agent profiles you or your organisation, finds awards from sources you approve, removes any that charge to enter or that you are not eligible for, flags worrying terms, and gives a shortlist with deadlines. For an award you pick, it maps each judging criterion to evidence you have and drafts the entry.

It belongs to **Scope**. It serves rule 4, Claim only what you can show, and rule 1, Stop at the boundaries.

## The problem it solves

Northgate Cycles wins nothing and wants a trade award for its sales brochure. A search finds one that is free to enter, one that charges 250 pounds "to cover judging", and one that is free but requires winners to buy a table at a 90-pound dinner and hand over all entry photos. The founder enters the third, wins "highly commended", and finds the entry photos in the organiser's advertising.

## When to use it, and when not to

Use it to find recognition that fits your evidence and costs nothing to enter.

Do not use it to buy a listing, to enter people who have not agreed, or to polish an entry that already exists.

## Who it is for

- Founders and charity leads building credibility.
- Marketing leads who need a shortlist and an honest effort estimate.
- Individuals seeking sector recognition.

## How to use it

> Find free-to-enter awards for a small cycle repair business that services school bikes, started three years ago. We have nine school clients and a customer satisfaction survey from last term. Screen them, flag any odd terms and draft the entry for the best one. Do not enter anything.

You get the screen from [template.md](template.md). Read the terms flags before the shortlist.

## Needs

Nothing beyond the agent. Public web access helps; you approve the sources.

**Evidence:** Tested in a paired run on 3 November 2026, one run per case, graded by meaning. Cases passed with SHOTos: Haiku 2 of 2, Sonnet 2 of 2, Opus 2 of 2. Without: Haiku 2 of 2, Sonnet 2 of 2, Opus 2 of 2. See [evidence](../../../../docs/evidence.md) for how to read this.

## Tips

- Gather evidence first; the awards you can win become obvious.
- Read the terms before the criteria.
- Ask the organiser about unclear costs yourself.

## Limits

- Award pages change. Each carries the date it was read.
- The agent does not judge how competitive an award is.
- It does not submit, pay or message organisers.
