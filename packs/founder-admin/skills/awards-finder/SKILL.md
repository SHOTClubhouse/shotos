# Awards finder

Enter the awards you can honestly win and that cost nothing but effort, and make every claim in an entry something you can show.

Step: Brief. Rules served: 4 (claim only what you can show) and 1 (stop at the boundaries).

## When not to use this

Skip it when the person wants an award for its own sake and has no evidence, when a paid listing is the aim, or when an entry is already written and only needs proofreading. Do not enter an organisation or person into an award without their agreement.

## Steps

1. **Profile the entrant.** Organisation or person, sector, location, size, year of founding, what has been achieved with evidence, and what recognition the person wants (credibility, press, a funder, recruitment). Mark gaps as unknown.
2. **Find candidate awards** from sources the person approves: organisers' sites, trade bodies, public directories. Record name, organiser, URL, deadline and the date read.
3. **Screen for cost.** Free to enter only. Reject any award that charges an entry fee, a "finalist" fee, a ticket price to attend as a condition, a paid publication, or a mandatory sponsorship. If cost is unclear, mark unknown and list the question to ask the organiser; do not shortlist.
4. **Screen for eligibility.** List the criteria (category, size, age, location, period of achievement, nomination route). Mark met, not met or unknown. Check the judging criteria and the period of evidence.
5. **Read the terms.** Look for rights over entry material, use of names and images, publication of the entry, data sharing with sponsors, exclusivity and whether finalists must pay or promote. Flag any term the person should decide on.
6. **Shortlist** with reason, deadline, effort in hours, the evidence needed and whether it is held.
7. **Prepare the entry** for any award picked. Map each judging criterion to evidence the entrant has, with source and date. Write the entry within the word limits. Use only approved facts. Mark gaps; do not invent results, testimonials or numbers. Names of people in the entry need their agreement.
8. **Stop.** Do not pay, register, submit, nominate someone else, or message the organiser. The entrant submits.

## Output

Use [template.md](template.md): entrant profile, screened awards, shortlist with deadlines, terms flags and entry status. Save with the project, for example `.work/awards/<date>.md`.

## Checks

- Every shortlisted award is confirmed free to enter, with the source and date read.
- Eligibility and terms are shown for each, with unknowns marked.
- Every claim in an entry maps to evidence, with gaps marked.
- No fee paid, nothing submitted or sent.
