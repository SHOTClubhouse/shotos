# Enablement plan: weekly workshop schedule

**Receiving team and sponsor:** Ines Calder (owner and sponsor), Tariq, Bo
**Accepts the exit result (name):** Ines Calder
**Builders:** Hana
**Window:** 7 November to 28 November, plus a four-week support period
**Time the team can give (per person, per week):** Ines 2 hours, Tariq 1 hour, Bo 90 minutes

## Exit criterion (agreed before training starts)
The team runs one full Friday cycle (export, draft, check, fix, publish) on 28 November, including a planted fault found and fixed, with Hana present as an observer and not answering unless asked to stop. Pass means: the schedule has no double bookings, no one is scheduled on holiday, Ines publishes it, and the planted fault is found by the team using the written steps. Ines confirms or does not.

The team should see Tariq appear free in the draft, trace it to the missing date and fix it using step 4 of the runbook. A pass means they do this without asking Hana.
