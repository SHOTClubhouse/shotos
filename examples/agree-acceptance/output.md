# Acceptance card

**Task:** one-page summary of last quarter's customer complaints for the board pack
**Owner (accepts the work):** the operations manager
**Date agreed:** waiting for confirmation

## Outcome
The board has a one-page, accurate view of last quarter's complaints and what is being done about them.

## Checks
1. Given the complaints log for the quarter, when the summary is written, then every number in it matches the log.
2. The summary fits on one A4 page and leads with the three largest complaint themes.
3. Each theme has an owner and a next action, or says "no action agreed".
4. No customer is named or identifiable.

## Must fail or refuse
If the complaints log for the quarter is incomplete or missing, stop and ask rather than estimating.

## Boundaries: ask the owner first
- Sending to the company secretary: **the agent drafts; the owner sends**, or gives an explicit yes first.
- Changing the complaints log.

## Confirmed
Waiting for the owner's yes.
