# Decision record 0001: Fixtures and availability app

**Status:** Decided
**Decider (name, role):** Rosa Quintero, treasurer (named in the 12 March minutes as the decider for purchases under 1,000 pounds a year)
**Date decided:** 14 March
**Review date and reviewer:** September, Rosa Quintero with the secretary

## Context
Millbrook Rovers youth club runs fixtures and availability through a spreadsheet and a group chat. The secretary spends about 5 hours a week on it (her estimate, not logged), and parents' phone numbers sit in a chat that any member can export. The club has 9 teams and expects 11 next season. The committee needed a choice before the season starts.

## Options considered
| Option | Gains | Costs | Main risk | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: spreadsheet plus group chat | Free; no change for anyone | Secretary's weekly hours stay as they are | Phone numbers exposed to export in the chat (raised by the welfare officer) | Yes |
| B: Fieldday | Alerts and availability in one place; data held in the UK according to the supplier's privacy page | 480 pounds a year for up to 12 teams | Data processing terms have not been read; supplier lock-in on parent contacts | Yes, within a year; moving contacts out needs checking |
| C: TeamBoard | Cheaper: 260 pounds for 6 teams, 410 for 12 | Still needs a chat for parent messages | Two tools to run; exposure in the chat remains | Yes |
| Do nothing | No spend | Same workload and exposure as A | Same as A | Not a stable choice |

## Decision
Use Fieldday (option B) for the coming season.

## Why
The February trial showed 31 of 36 parents answering availability requests inside the app, and the club needs messaging as well as fixtures, which C does not provide. The cost for 11 teams is within the treasurer's limit. The downside accepted: the supplier's data processing terms were not read before the decision, so the welfare concern is open and moves into the actions below.

## Evidence
| Fact | Source and date | Measured, inferred or opinion |
|---|---|---|
| 31 of 36 trial parents responded in the app | February trial, two teams, three weeks | Measured |
| No count of group chat responses before the trial | Committee pack | Gap: no baseline |
| 5 hours a week on fixtures and availability | Secretary, last season | Opinion (her estimate, not logged) |
| 7 of 9 managers said they would use an app | Show of hands, 12 March | Measured, small sample |
| Data stored in the UK | Fieldday privacy page | Inferred: supplier's statement, terms not read |
| Price 480 pounds for up to 12 teams | Supplier quote | Measured |

Dissenting views: Lewis Barrett (chair) preferred option C to save money. Femi Adeyemi (welfare officer) supported B only if the data terms are checked.

## What would change this
- The data processing terms are unacceptable to the welfare officer, or the supplier cannot export parents' contacts on request.
- Fewer than two thirds of parents respond to availability requests in the app after the first six weeks of the season.
- The club grows beyond 12 teams or the price rises above 1,000 pounds a year.
