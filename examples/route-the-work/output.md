## 2. Apology to 38 customers
`Asset: apology message plus a refund-or-exchange offer, one draft and a 38-row send list from data/wrong-orders.csv | Job: each affected customer knows what went wrong and can choose a refund or the right size | Risk: high, tags money and external and personal-data | Reader: 38 customers, who will act on the offer | Route: a skill, then an independent reviewer | Proof: three test copies read by you, then the draft checked against the CSV by a second agent, then a staged send of three before the rest`

Tags and evidence:
| Tag | Evidence | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| money | The message offers refunds, and the CSV has a price per row | The offer wording and each amount need your yes |
| external | 38 customer email addresses in the CSV | Nothing is sent without your yes. I draft; you send, or tell me to |
| personal-data | Customer emails and order details in the CSV | The draft uses first names and order numbers only, and the data stays in the project |

I will not send it today without your yes. I will write the draft, check every amount against the CSV, ask a second agent from a different runtime to review the draft and the numbers, then show you both. If you want it sent, the first send is three customers, then we look, then the rest. See pilot-before-scale.

What would make this lighter: if you tell me the message is a plain apology with no refund offer, the `money` tag goes and an independent reviewer is not needed.

First yes needed from you: the wording and the offer, before anything is sent.
