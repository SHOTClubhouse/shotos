# What the new fee bands change

**Reader:** the committee. **Question:** do the new age bands cost families more? **Claim to prove or disprove:** "the bands do not cost families more". **Source:** `fees.xlsx`, sheets 1 to 3, register export dated 3 November.

**Level chosen:** 2, a before-and-after table. The question is about amounts, so a table answers it. A calculator page (level 4) would only be worth building if the committee needed to try many family shapes themselves; four examples show the pattern, and the rule under the table covers every family. I added the table as a new sheet, "Comparison", in `fees.xlsx`. I did not make a copy of the file.

## Four sample families

These families are **illustrative**: made up to show the pattern. The register does not record families, so I could not use real ones. The fees are literal.

| Family (illustrative) | Children | Now | Proposed | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | two under 12 | 120 | 80 | 40 less |
| B | one 12 to 15, one over 15 | 120 | 125 | 5 more |
| C | two under 12, two over 15 | 240 | 220 | 20 less |
| D | one over 15 | 60 | 70 | 10 more |

**What to notice:** each child over 15 costs 10 pounds more, each child aged 12 to 15 costs 5 pounds less and each child under 12 costs 20 pounds less, so the club would collect 745 pounds less a term.

- **What it proves:** the claim does not hold for every family. A family pays more exactly when 10 pounds for each child over 15 comes to more than 20 pounds for each child under 12 plus 5 pounds for each child aged 12 to 15. Having a child under 12 is not enough: a family with one child under 12 and three over 15 pays 250 pounds instead of 240 (literal fees; the families are illustrative).
- **What was inferred:** nothing. Every total is computed from the sheets. I checked each total twice by recomputing it in the sheet.
- **Decision now needed:** whether the committee accepts a 745 pound fall in fee income each term, or adjusts a band.

## Not checked

- Whether the register export of 3 November includes members who joined since.
- How many real families meet that rule and so would pay more. The register cannot show it.
