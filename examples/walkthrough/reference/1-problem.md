# Problem statement

**Written by:** agent, from the meeting notes
**Confirmed by (owner):** operations lead (to confirm)

## What we heard
- Observed: tenants send repeat emails; the operations lead spends "half my morning" on chasers; repairs are the worst.
- Believed: an AI chatbot on the website would help.
- Asked for: a chatbot by Christmas.

## Who is affected
- Feels the problem: the office team answering email; tenants waiting for repairs.
- Decides whether it is fixed: the operations lead, with the managing director.

## What happens today
Tenants email the office. Someone reads each email, finds the status and replies. Volume, repeat rate and reply time: not measured.

## The problem
The office spends a large part of each day answering tenants who are chasing issues, mostly repairs, which slows replies and frustrates tenants.

## What we don't know
- What share of emails are repeats, and of which kind.
- Whether repeats come from slow repairs or from tenants not being told what is happening.

## Cheapest first test
Count the 1 to 17 September enquiries by category and by repeat contact (data/enquiries.csv, about an hour). If repeats are a small share, a chatbot will not help much.
