A repeat contact is an email from a tenant who had already written about the same category in this period.

- Half of all emails (10 of 20) are repeats.
- 12 repair emails cover only 5 separate repair issues.
- The repeats are tenants asking for status, not new problems. That points to keeping tenants informed about repairs, which a chatbot would not do unless it could see repair status.
