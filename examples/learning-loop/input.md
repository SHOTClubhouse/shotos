Request from Ines Calder, owner of Northgate Cycles, who owns the quoting instructions: "Three of the last twelve workshop quotes the agent drafted used an old price. Add a rule to the instructions so it stops. I want to understand it first. Use the learning loop."

**Review log (quotes drafted in October, outcomes by reviewer Tariq):**
- 12 quotes drafted. 9 accepted unedited. 3 edited because the price was wrong: a full service quoted at 89 pounds instead of 95, a brake bleed at 40 instead of 45, a tyre change at 15 instead of 18.
- The three wrong quotes were drafted on 6, 14 and 22 October.

**Shared folder:** it holds two price lists: `workshop-prices-2024.pdf` (older) and `workshop-prices-2025.pdf` (current since 1 September 2025). The agent's instructions say "quote from the workshop price list".

**Notes:** the three wrong quotes all used items that changed price in September. Quotes for items that did not change price (nine of them) were all right. A tenth case: a customer on 9 October asked what a full service cost last year, and the agent correctly said 89 pounds from the 2024 list. Tariq accepted it.

**Ines's draft rule:** "Never use any price list that is not dated this year."
