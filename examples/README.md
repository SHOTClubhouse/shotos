# Examples

## Practise with fictional work

Choose a job and run it in your usual assistant or with the installed skills. Give the assistant the brief and input records. Keep the review notes aside until you have a result. In exercises with several rounds, share only the records for the current round.

| Job | Exercise | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decide what to build | [Workshop booking discovery](../docs/guides/startup-founder.md#try-a-fictional-discovery-exercise) | The founder tests confirmation ownership first, then reads a headline result alongside the agreed stop condition. |
| Win a deal and pass it to delivery | [Proposal to customer handoff](../packs/win-the-work/skills/contextual-proposal/worked-deal.md) | Buyer acceptance and receiving lead acceptance have separate evidence in the same deal record. |
| Review a recurring leadership decision | [Executive Decision Review](../packs/exec-ops/skills/decision-brief/review-cycle.md#try-a-worked-scenario) | The decision maker, handoff and next independent cycle each have evidence. |
| Prepare a supplier invitation and rubric | [Booking tool RFP](../packs/win-the-work/skills/rfp-authoring/review-exercise.md) | The panel scores a comparable response and keeps the mandatory export check separate. |
| Reconcile conflicting research | [Supplier support research](../skills/research-and-assemble/source-authority-example.md) | A repeated headline is traced to its original source, and the applicable schedule governs the brief. |
| Recover a partial executive brief | [Daily Brief recovery](../packs/exec-ops/skills/daily-brief/recovery-example.md) | The checkpoint survives an outage and the retry matches the existing ledger row. |
| Hand over an operational pilot | [Invoice-query exercise](../docs/guides/invoice-query-exercise.md) | A reviewer checks exceptions, a receiving lead runs independently, and the next site starts with its own baseline. |
| Build and verify a public preview | [Northbank club website](northbank-club/README.md) | Visitors can use the home and open-day journeys and the public revision matches the intended release. |
| Prepare a campaign and hand over its review | [Northbank campaign review](northbank-club/campaign-review.md) | The page and artwork carry one approved action. A venue change triggers a fresh check and the operator records which revision can go forward. |

For the full six-stage sequence, follow the [Harbourside walkthrough](../docs/walkthrough.md).
