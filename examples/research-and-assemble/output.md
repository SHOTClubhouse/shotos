# Briefing: should Northgate Cycles sell e-bikes?

For the supplier meeting on Thursday. Built from the three files in `research/` only. No public pages were searched, because none were added by the time of writing; see the gaps.

## What the sources support
- UK e-bike sales rose 12% in 2023, to 224,000 units, while conventional adult bike sales fell 4% [1]. This is from March 2024. It is two years old and says nothing about 2024 or 2025.
- Our own sales last year were 1,940 bikes, none of them e-bikes, because we have not stocked them [3]. We therefore have no sales data of our own to say what our customers would buy.

## What is inferred
- If the 2023 trend continued, the e-bike share of the market has grown since [1]. This is an inference from one year's figure, not a finding. Nothing in the files shows what happened after 2023.
- Our road, gravel, hybrid and kids sales may be exposed to the shift away from conventional bikes [1, 3]. The release gives an overall fall of 4% for conventional adult bikes. It does not break down the categories we sell. Reasoning: the same direction, not the same size.

## What is not established
- How much range customers want. One forum poster says 100 km, two agree and one says 60 km is enough for a 20 km commute [2]. This is four opinions from one thread and does not show what buyers want. Treat it as a question for the supplier, not a requirement.

## Questions for the supplier
1. What is the real-world range of each model, and how is it measured?
2. What minimum order and payment terms apply, and can we start with a small test range?
3. What is the warranty on the battery, and who handles claims?
4. Who services the bikes, and what training or tools do we need?
5. What have comparable independent shops sold per model in their first year? (Ask for names you can check; do not rely on their summary.)

## Gaps
- No data on e-bike demand after 2023, and nothing on our local area.
- No competitor prices or margins.
- No evidence of customers asking us for e-bikes. A count of enquiries in the last year would help; it is in your inbox, not in these files.

## Source list
[1] Cycling trade body press release, 14 March 2024, `research/trade-body-release-2024.txt`. Secondary: a trade body's summary of sales.
[2] Forum thread, `research/forum-thread.txt`. Unknown reliability: four anonymous opinions.
[3] Northgate Cycles sales for 2025, `research/our-sales-2025.csv`. Primary for our sales.
