# Rollout plan: repair-reply drafting to Quayside and Millfield

**Sponsor (approves):** Priya Nair, head of lettings operations
**Basis:** Evidence pack of 3 November. Scale decision recorded on 12 November by Alan Whitcombe, managing director: extend one more quarter at Eastgate, then roll out. The extension ended on 10 February.
**What worked, in what conditions, what is unknown:** At Eastgate, which has a clean helpdesk export, median first reply fell from 26 hours to 5.4 and replies within 4 hours rose from 18 per cent to 49 per cent, with about 40 per cent of drafts edited. Staff time saved was never measured, and the 4-hour target of 60 per cent was not reached. Whether this holds in a branch with a different mix of repairs, or with no log, is unknown.

Status: draft for Priya Nair. Nothing has been sent to any branch.

| Order | Team | Why this one | What differs from Eastgate | Main risk | Start (draft date) |

| 2 | Millfield | Smaller branch, long lets | No repair log or export | No baseline to compare with | Start logging repairs now; pilot decision after eight weeks of data; start no earlier than June |

The managing director's date of June for both is feasible only for starting Millfield, not for measuring it. Say so to the sponsor: a result needs a baseline period of at least eight weeks before the pilot.

Checkpoint before Millfield starts: Quayside's evidence pack, and a Millfield baseline from eight weeks of logging.

## Governance
Priya Nair decides on starts, pauses and any change to the boundary list. Each branch lead may change their contractor list, tone settings and local eval cases. A branch asks Priya to change a shared skill; changes go through the gate in [skill-quality-gate](../../skills/skill-quality-gate/README.md). A stop is recorded as a short note with the reason and the date.

## Improvement cadence
A 45-minute slot every four weeks with the three branch leads and Jaswinder: share the measures, bring lessons to the learning loop, and agree skill changes. Priya owns the shared copy of the skill. Branch changes go in as proposals.

## Checkpoints and stop
After each branch: an evidence pack and a decision to continue, adapt or pause. Pause the whole rollout if a reply with a wrong contractor, date or cost is sent to a tenant, or if a branch cannot meet its baseline period.
