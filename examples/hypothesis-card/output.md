# Hypothesis card

**Idea:** a website chatbot that answers repair-status questions
**Owner (decides after the time box):** the owner of Harbourside Lettings
**Date:** before April

## The belief
We believe that **adding a repair-status chatbot to the website** will result in **fewer repair-status phone calls from tenants**, and we will know because **repair-status calls per week** changes from **not measured (see below)** to **at least 30% lower than the 4-week count taken before launch** within **12 weeks of launch, ending 30 June**.

## Measure
| Measure | Definition and unit | Where it is read | Baseline | Target | Time box ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Repair-status calls per week | Incoming calls whose main reason is "where is my repair", counted by staff with a tally sheet | Office tally sheet, plus the phone system report for total volume | Not measured. The office manager's guess is "about a third of 1,620 calls last month", which is an unchecked estimate, not a baseline | At least 30% below the pre-launch 4-week average (the 30% is a proposal for the owner to change; no evidence yet says it is achievable) | 30 June |

Before launch, count calls for 4 weeks. The chatbot launch moves to May if the owner wants the baseline first. The alternative is to launch in April and drop the "30% fewer calls" claim, keeping only the chatbot usage count.

## Guard measure (must not get worse)
| Measure | Current value | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Repair jobs reported late or twice because a tenant could not get through | Not measured | Do not rise above the pre-launch tally. Count them on the same sheet |
| Engaged-tone complaints from tenants | Not measured; the owner reports they happen | Do not rise |

## Stop result
Stop and cancel the 90 pounds a month plan if, at six weeks, fewer than 1 in 5 tenant visits to the repair-status page start a chat, or at 12 weeks the weekly repair-status call count is not at least 15% below the pre-launch average. Either result means tenants are not using it or it is not replacing calls. Stop at once if the chatbot ever gives a tenant a wrong status, and tell the owner.

## Assumptions
| Assumption | Status | Cheapest way to check |
|---|---|---|
| Around a third of calls are repair-status calls | Unchecked | Tally the next 40 calls by reason (one week) |
| Tenants will use a chatbot instead of phoning | Unknown | Add a "check status online" link to the repairs email and count clicks before buying anything |
| The repairs log is current enough to answer from | Unchecked. Updated each afternoon, so morning answers may be a day old | Compare 20 log entries with the real job state |
| The chatbot will not show one tenant another tenant's repair | Unchecked | Ask the supplier how it identifies the tenant before any trial |

## Not authorised by this card
Signing the quote, connecting the chatbot to the repairs log, or contacting tenants. Each needs the owner's yes.
