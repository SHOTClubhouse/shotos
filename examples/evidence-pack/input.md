# Input

All names and numbers below are invented for this example.

Request from the managing director of Harbourside Lettings:

"The repair-reply pilot finished on 31 October. I need to decide whether to roll it out to the two other branches. Give me the evidence pack. Everyone says it went well."

Material in the project folder:

**From the Scope problem statement (signed off 2 July by Priya Nair, head of lettings operations):**
- Baseline, first reply to a repair request, median: 26 hours (helpdesk export, 1 April to 30 June, 1,412 requests).
- Baseline, requests answered within 4 hours: 18 per cent (same export).
- Baseline, tenant complaints about slow replies: 11 in the quarter (complaints log).
- Staff time on repair replies: not measured.

**From the acceptance card (agreed 16 July):**
- Success: median first reply under 8 hours and at least 60 per cent answered within 4 hours.
- Draft replies sent without staff editing: no threshold set.
- Any reply that gives a wrong contractor, date or cost counts as a failure.

**Helpdesk export, 1 August to 31 October (1,003 requests):**
- Median first reply: 5.5 hours.
- Answered within 4 hours: 47 per cent.
- Note from the export owner: October volume was lower than August and September because a large block of flats was handed to another agent on 1 October.

**Complaints log, same period:** 4 complaints about slow replies.

**Pilot notes from the team (messages, not exports):**
- "Staff edit about half the drafts."
- "We found two replies that named the wrong contractor. Both were caught before sending."
- "It saves loads of time."

**Cost:** licence and set-up invoice of 1,850 pounds, total, from the finance ledger.
