# Run a partner pilot

Use this workflow when two organisations have agreed to try something and the next step depends on several people. Start with the accepted deal or written agreement. Keep it as the source for commitments, and use the [programme record](../../templates/programme.md) to link decisions, evidence and ownership across the pilot.

| When | Who owns the next decision | What to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define the pilot | The commercial owner confirms the agreed scope with the partner. | Accepted revision, scope, baseline and exclusions. Use [Contextual Proposal](../../packs/win-the-work/skills/contextual-proposal/README.md) if terms are still being drafted. |
| Agree how to judge it | The pilot owner and sponsor agree the measure and stop conditions. | An [acceptance card](../../templates/acceptance.md) with a threshold, comparison period, evidence source and review date. |
| Decide whether to start | The pilot owner confirms staffing and access. The authorised owner approves the live activity and the communications owner reviews any public copy separately. | Named leads, permissions, dependencies and the launch decision. Use [Pilot Before Scale](../../skills/pilot-before-scale/README.md) and [Claims Audit](../../packs/trust/skills/claims-audit/README.md). |
| Review the result | The sponsor decides whether to proceed to Own, change the pilot, hold or stop. | An [evidence pack](../../skills/evidence-pack/README.md) and a dated [decision record](../../skills/decision-record/README.md). |
| Transfer ownership | The receiving lead confirms access and accepts the handoff. They then run a cycle independently before the sponsor considers expansion. | The agreed first action, [handoff receipt](../../packs/customer-success/skills/success-handoff/README.md) and separate [independent-cycle evidence](../../skills/enablement-plan/README.md). |

## Try it with a fictional partner

Start with the [North Quay worked deal](../../packs/win-the-work/skills/contextual-proposal/worked-deal.md). The club accepted proposal P2 and Robin accepted the delivery handoff. The baseline is two of four registers complete by Monday. The club still controls access to its register.

Use the following fictional updates in order. After each one, update the programme record with the decision, the evidence and the next owner.

1. **9 November.** Morgan has yet to name the register lead. The parties agree to judge the four-session pilot against three of four registers completed by Monday. The sponsor asks whether the team can start.
2. **13 November.** Morgan confirms the lead, setup and permitted access. The authorised sponsor approves the pilot. The partner also asks for a public announcement, but the communications owner has yet to approve copy.
3. **9 December.** The club records three of four registers complete by Monday. The sponsor accepts the result for the pilot group and authorises an independent cycle with the receiving lead.

> Use the partner pilot workflow and programme record. Cite the accepted agreement and the new evidence for each decision. Show the current stage, who owns the next action, and which permissions are still pending. Keep simulated results labelled as fictional.
