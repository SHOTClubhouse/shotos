# Sources

SHOTos builds on public work. These are the ideas it draws on most.

- Continuous delivery: Jez Humble and David Farley, *Continuous Delivery* (2010).
- Software delivery performance: DORA research programme and *Accelerate* (Forsgren, Humble, Kim, 2018). [dora.dev](https://dora.dev)
- Product over project: Mik Kersten, *Project to Product* (2018).
- Team design and cognitive load: Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais, *Team Topologies* (2019).
- Specification by example and BDD: Gojko Adzic, *Specification by Example* (2011); Dan North, "Introducing BDD" (2006).
- Lean and agile practice: the Agile Manifesto (2001); Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup* (2011).
- Evaluating AI systems: Anthropic, "Demystifying evals for AI agents"; OpenAI agent evaluation guides.
- Keeping people in control of AI: NIST AI Risk Management Framework; ISO/IEC 42001; EU AI Act, Article 14 on human oversight.
- Skill format: the Agent Skills specification. [agentskills.io](https://agentskills.io/specification)
- Maturity maps: Steve Yegge's eight levels, via [Augment Code](https://www.augmentcode.com/guides/steve-yegge-8-levels-ai-assisted-development); [n8n AI maturity framework](https://blog.n8n.io/ai-maturity-the-5-level-framework/).
