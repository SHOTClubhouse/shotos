# Write a decision record

Capture a decision while the reasons are fresh, in one page, so that later readers can see what was chosen, why, and what would reopen it.

Step: any, most often Check. Rules: 4 Claim only what you can show, 1 Stop at the boundaries.

## When not to use this

Skip it for a trivial or easily reversed choice that no one will ask about. Do not use it to make the decision: the agent records a decision a named person has made or drafts options for them to choose between.

## Steps

1. **Name the decider.** One person owns the decision, by name and role. If it is not clear who decides, ask before drafting. A group can advise; one person decides.
2. **State the context.** Two to five sentences: the situation, the constraint, the deadline and what happens if nothing is decided. Link to the problem statement or evidence pack rather than repeating it.
3. **List the options.** At least two real options, plus "do nothing" where it is a real choice. For each give what it costs, what it gains, the main risk and how reversible it is. Include the option that was rejected first and why.
4. **Record the decision.** One sentence in plain words. If the decider has not decided yet, write "Draft, awaiting the decider" and give a recommendation labelled as such.
5. **Attach the evidence.** List the facts that carried the decision, each with a source and date. Mark which are measured, which are inferred and which are opinion. Keep opinions that disagreed with the outcome and say who held them.
6. **Say what would change it.** Name at least one observable trigger: a number crossing a line, a cost rise, a supplier change, a failed test. This is the contrary case for the decision.
7. **Set a review date.** Pick a date or an event after which someone looks again. Say who reviews.
8. **Note what this supersedes or depends on.** Link earlier records. Mark the older one "superseded by" with the new one's number.
9. **Check authority.** If the decision spends money, sends something outside, deletes or publishes, the record is not the approval. Add an "Actions still needing a yes" line.

## Output

Use [template.md](template.md). Save in the project's decisions folder, numbered, for example `decisions/0007-choose-booking-supplier.md`. Never edit an accepted record to change the outcome; write a new one that supersedes it.

## Checks

- A named person is recorded as decider, and the record does not say "the team decided".
- At least two options with a downside for the chosen one.
- Evidence items have sources and are labelled measured, inferred or opinion.
- A change trigger and a review date are present.
- The agent has not recorded a decision the decider did not make.
