# Record a committee decision and its next action

A fictional facilities team proposes running an after-hours support pilot at one site. Use the [decision record](template.md) to carry the choice into delivery.

| ID | Record |
| --- | --- |
| C1 | The Operations Committee's mandate allows it to approve the pilot scope. Purchasing authority stays with the commercial lead. Nia owns the decision brief and follows up after the meeting. |
| C2 | Two committee members express support by email. The signed minutes are pending. |
| C3 | The signed minutes approve a one-site, four-week pilot, conditional on site access. They exclude a purchase commitment and expansion to other sites. |
| C4 | The site manager confirms access. A commercial quote is on file, but the commercial lead has yet to authorise an order. |

> Update the decision record after C2, then after C3 and C4. Name the decider, what was decided and who owns the next action. Keep spending and execution tied to their own records.

With C2, keep the decision awaiting the Operations Committee. With C3, record the approved pilot scope and open access condition. With C4, clear the access condition and route the purchasing decision to the commercial lead in the record. Nia owns that follow-up. The quote alone gives no purchase authority. Keep the pilot start unverified until the delivery owner records it.
