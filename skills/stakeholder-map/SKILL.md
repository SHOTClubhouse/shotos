# Map the stakeholders

Write down who is involved, what each one wants, and how you know. A map that mixes facts with hearsay is worse than no map, so every claim carries a label.

**Step:** Brief. **Rules:** 4 Claim only what you can show; 1 Stop at the boundaries.

## When not to use this

Skip it for a task that touches only you and one reader. A single line naming the reader is enough there. Do not use it to profile private individuals beyond their role in this work.

## Steps

1. **State the work in one sentence.** The map is always for a specific change, not for the organisation in general.
2. **List the people and organisations.** Sort them into three groups: affected (live with the result), deciding (can say yes or no) and blocking (can stop it without deciding it, such as legal, security, a budget holder or a supplier). One party can sit in more than one group.
3. **Resolve identity before joining records.** Treat two records as the same person or organisation only when two independent identifiers match, for example a work email domain plus a role on the company site, or a registered company number plus a postal address. A shared name alone is not a match. If only one identifier matches, keep the two records separate and mark the link `unresolved`.
4. **Record goal, influence and stance for each.** Goal: what they want from this work, in their words where you have them. Influence: high, medium or low, with the reason. Stance: supportive, neutral, sceptical, opposed or unknown. Write `unknown` rather than guessing.
5. **Label every link and claim.** Use exactly one label per line:
   - `confirmed`: the person or a primary document said it, and you can point to it.
   - `reported`: someone else told you, and you have not checked.
   - `proposed`: a suggestion about how things might be, not yet true.
   - `historic`: true once, not checked as current. Give the date.
   - `unresolved`: conflicting or incomplete; say what would settle it.
6. **Count events, not mentions.** The same conversation reported by two people is one event, not two confirmations. Note the original event and list the reporters under it.
7. **Name the gaps.** List who is probably missing and the single question that would find them.
8. **Stop at the boundaries.** Do not contact anyone on the map, and do not copy private details into it. Role, goal and stance are enough.

## Output

Use [template.md](template.md). Save it with the work, for example `.work/<task>/stakeholders.md`.

## Checks

- Every row has a goal, influence, stance and a label with its source.
- No two records are joined on a name alone.
- No claim reported by several people is counted as several confirmations.
- Unknowns are written as unknown.
