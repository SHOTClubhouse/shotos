## Preflight checklist
- [ ] Inputs: list is final, counted, deduplicated, from the right source
- [ ] Targets: results go to the right place (live, not a copy, or the reverse)
- [ ] Rollback: how to undo it, tested on a pilot item; or "cannot be undone" with the owner's yes
- [ ] Budget: time and money, and the limit at which the run pauses
- [ ] Stop condition: what halts the run, and who is told
- [ ] External actions (send, post, buy, delete, publish) have the owner's yes
