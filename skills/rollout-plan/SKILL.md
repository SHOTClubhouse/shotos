# Plan the rollout to the next team

Scale the method, not the result. Each new team gets what worked, then measures its own starting point and proves its own value.

Step: Improve. Rules: 1 Stop at the boundaries, 3 Read back the real result, 4 Claim only what you can show. Control 9, pilot-before-scale.

## When not to use this

Skip it when there was no decision to scale, or the first team is not yet self-sufficient (see [enablement-plan](../enablement-plan/README.md)). Do not use it to announce a rollout: the plan is a draft until the sponsor agrees.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the basis.** Link the evidence pack and the recorded scale decision, with the decider's name. If there is neither, stop and say the plan is premature. Restate in two sentences what worked, in what conditions, and what is unknown.
2. **List what travels.** Separate the method (steps, rules, skills, templates, eval sets, the runbook), from the settings (thresholds, names, data sources, owners) and from the assumptions (the first team's workload, data quality, people, tools). Mark each as copy, adapt or re-establish.
3. **Choose the sequence.** Order the next teams by readiness and value, not by who asked first. Start with three units or fewer, then check before widening. If asked to go to every team at once, say that the first wave is three or fewer and why, and that the rest follow a checkpoint. For each team: why this one, what is different from the first team, and the main risk.
4. **Decide who moves with it.** Name the people from the first team who will support (a coach, a champion, a reviewer), the share of time they give and the end date. Build in train-the-trainer so each new team has its own people who can teach.
5. **Re-baseline in each new team.** Before starting, each team measures its own starting point with the same method as the brief: the problem statement, the baseline measures, the data checks and the acceptance. Do not carry the first team's figures forward as if they were this team's.
6. **Run a preflight.** For each team, check access, data quality, owners, the boundary list and the stop conditions before any agent task runs. List what blocks starting.
7. **Set the governance.** Who decides at each step, what each team may change locally and what only the sponsor may change, how a team asks to deviate, and how stops are recorded. Keep the boundary list intact.
8. **Set the improvement cadence.** A regular slot (for example every four weeks) where the teams share evidence, bring lessons to the [learning-loop](../learning-loop/README.md) and update shared skills through the gate. Say who owns the shared copy.
9. **Define the checkpoints and the stop.** After each team: an evidence pack, a decision to continue, adapt or pause. Say what would pause the whole rollout, such as a material failure or a team that cannot meet its baseline.
10. **Present as a draft.** The sponsor approves the sequence and the people. The agent does not announce, schedule meetings, or contact teams.

## Output

Use [template.md](template.md). Save as `.work/<task>/rollout-plan.md`.

## Checks

- The plan links an evidence pack and a named scale decision.
- It separates method, settings and assumptions, and each new team has a re-baseline step.
- Three or fewer units go first, with a checkpoint before more.
- People who move with the method and their time and end date are named.
- No announcement, invitation or message has been sent.
