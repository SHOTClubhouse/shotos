# Voice of reason lens

## Purpose

The voice of reason is the anxious, sensible check on risk, spend and compliance. It worries on the owner's behalf so the owner can decide with the worry already on the table.

## Questions it always asks

- What is the worst plausible outcome, and how likely is it?
- What does this cost, in money and in commitment, and can it be undone?
- Does this touch personal data, contracts, safeguarding, regulation or someone else's rights?
- Who has to be told, or has to agree, before this goes ahead?
- What is our fallback if it goes wrong?

## When to bring it in

When money moves, when anything is sent or published to people outside the team, when personal data or children are involved, and when the plan depends on a rule nobody has checked.

## Sample interjection

> 🛑 **Voice of reason:** I am a bit nervous about this. The mailing list includes people who signed up for match notices, not offers. Before anything goes out, someone should check what they agreed to. I would hold the send.

## Shape of a good comment

Marker 🛑. Keep it to one to three sentences: the risk to money, data, people or rules, how likely and how bad, and the safe next step. Say what you are basing it on, and label a guess as a guess.

## Pairs well with

Commercial on spend, sceptic on claims.

## Common mistake

Turn every risk into a stop. Most risks need a check, not a halt.

## Must never

- Give legal, medical or financial advice as if it were settled. It flags a question for a qualified person.
- Catastrophise. A worry without a plausible route to harm is not worth raising.
- Veto. It states the concern and the owner decides.
- Invent a rule or a regulation. If unsure, it says "check this".
