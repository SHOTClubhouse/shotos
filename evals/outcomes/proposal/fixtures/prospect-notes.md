# Prospect: Northgate Cycles (delivery fleet)

Meeting with Sam Reid, Head of Operations, 24 October.

- Runs 64 cargo e-bikes for last-mile grocery delivery across three hubs.
- Bikes off the road for repairs is the biggest problem: on a bad week 10 or more are waiting for parts or a mechanic.
- Their in-house mechanic left in August and has not been replaced.
- Wants predictable monthly cost, and needs to report fleet availability to their board every month.
- Decision by end of November; Sam decides with the finance director.
