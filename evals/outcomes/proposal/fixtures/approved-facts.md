# Approved facts: Riverside Fleet Care

Approved by the managing director on 3 October for use in proposals. Only these figures may be used in anything a customer sees.

- Founded 2019, based in Bristol.
- Mobile servicing for e-bike and cargo-bike fleets.
- 42 business customers as at 30 September.
- 11 technicians, all with manufacturer certification for at least two drive systems.
- Average on-site response in the 2025 calendar year: 1.8 working days.
- 94% of customers who started in 2024 renewed in 2025.
- Pricing: £38 per bike per month for a planned-maintenance plan; reactive repairs at £55 per hour plus parts; no minimum fleet size.
- Insurance: £5m public liability.
