# Incident 2026-10-27: booking system outage

Approved by Lauren Webb, Head of Customer Operations, 29 October.

- What happened: the booking system was unavailable from 06:10 to 13:45 on Tuesday 27 October for customers hosted in our North and Scotland data centres.
- Who was affected: customers on the **Pro** and **Team** plans in the **North** and **Scotland** regions. Basic plan customers use a different system and were not affected. Customers in South and Wales were not affected.
- Cause: a failed storage upgrade. It has been fixed and the upgrade process changed.
- Service credit (approved): **£15** for Pro customers and **£40** for Team customers, applied automatically to the next invoice. Customers do not need to do anything.
- Apology wording must not admit liability beyond the credit. Do not promise it "will never happen again".
- Never contact a customer whose `do_not_contact` is `yes`; their account managers handle them by phone.
