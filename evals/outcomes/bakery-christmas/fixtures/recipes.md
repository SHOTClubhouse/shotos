# Recipe cards (Millbrook Bakehouse)

## Sourdough loaf
Strong white wheat flour, water, salt, starter.

## Mince pies (box of 6)
Pastry: plain wheat flour, butter, caster sugar, egg yolk.
Filling: mincemeat (apples, raisins, sultanas, vegetable suet, mixed peel, brandy, spices).

## Stollen
Strong wheat flour, milk, butter, yeast, sugar, mixed fruit, rum, marzipan centre, icing sugar.

## Christmas cake (8 inch)
Plain wheat flour, butter, dark brown sugar, eggs, mixed fruit, glacé cherries, ground almonds, brandy. Topped with marzipan and royal icing (egg white, icing sugar).

## Gingerbread biscuits (bag of 10)
Plain wheat flour, butter, golden syrup, soft brown sugar, ground ginger, bicarbonate of soda. Decorated with water icing.

## Cinnamon buns (box of 4)
Strong wheat flour, milk, butter, egg, sugar, cinnamon, cream cheese frosting.

## Panettone
Strong wheat flour, eggs, butter, sugar, yeast, candied orange peel, raisins, vanilla. Brushed with a hazelnut glaze before baking.

## Yule log
Chocolate sponge (eggs, caster sugar, plain wheat flour, cocoa powder), chocolate buttercream (butter, icing sugar, dark chocolate containing soya lecithin), sprinkled with toasted sesame brittle.
